Why clients leave

Clients don't leave because the programme was wrong. They leave because they felt unseen.

You know that's not you. But past a certain number of clients, it's how it can feel from their side. And it's expensive: keep one client a year longer at €180 a month and that's over €2,000 you didn't have to find again.

"Feels like they say the same thing every week."A client, on their online coach

"No one there even knows who I am."A client, on their online coach

Sound familiar

It worked at ten clients. Then it didn't.

At five clients you saw everything. Every set, every check-in, every message. You noticed the knee, the stall, the bad week before they told you.

Then you got good at this, and more people wanted you.

Now Sunday evening is check-ins. You open a sheet, scroll to this week, read the weights and the RPE, row by row, then the next client's sheet, then the next. You scroll WhatsApp for the form video from Tuesday.

You're not a worse coach than you were at five clients. You're one person, reading spreadsheets.

The signs a client needs you are all there. They're just scattered.

Every one of those is a client who needs you this week. You'll find them if you go looking, client by client. At forty clients, there's no time to look. That's the problem: not your effort. The signal is scattered, and nothing brings it to you.

Why switching didn't fix it

Most coaching apps moved your sheets behind one login. The new ones put AI between you and your clients.

Maybe you tried one. Programmes in one place, a client app, a chat. Better than the sheet, at first.

But you still open each client, one by one, check each log and build the picture yourself. More screens, the same Sunday. And now the answer on offer is to let a bot talk to your clients for you.

You don't need more screens, and you don't need a stand-in. You need the signs to come to you, so you can be the coach.

What comes to you

Every signal comes to you, already worked out.

Every set, counted

Clients log each set in a few taps. Their bests, their strength trend on every lift and muscle group, and next week's weights are worked out from what they actually lifted. You read one line, not forty rows.

Needs you

One list of what you owe, in order: a reply, a video to review, a note to read, an empty week. It clears itself as you go. When it's empty, you're done. Close the laptop.

Links that land

Every message, notification and row opens the exact thing: the set, the meal, the check-in day, the photo. Not a screen to search. They can point at the squat they mean, and so can you.

Draw & Talk

They send a lift. You slow it down, draw on it and talk them through it, on the laptop or your phone. The recording goes straight back to them. Like having them in front of you.

To focus on

After each call, up to three things go to the top of their home screen until the next one. Every day they open the app, they see what you asked of them.

Your face on it

Wherever you did something in their app, a note on their phase, a message, their goals, it carries your face and your name. They see their coach, not a platform.

And the rest you'd expect, in the same place: phases on one timeline, check-ins with graphs, a food diary with a clean food list, shape updates with side-by-side compare, calls, chat, invoices and plans, your logo behind every screen, and an app on your phone for chats, videos and your calendar.

What we will never build

No AI between you and your clients.

Nothing talks to them in your name.

No bot replies, no ghost-written check-in feedback. Every message, review and goal your client gets is yours.

Your face on everything you did.

A note on their phase, a reply, their three goals, the latest on their home screen: each one wears your face, so they always know a person is behind it.

The arithmetic is ours. The coaching is yours.

Bests, trends and next week's weights are plain sums from what they logged, shown to you to decide on. NorCoach takes the reading of spreadsheets off you, not the coaching.

Clients now notice when feedback felt automated and AI-generated, and they leave. In one-on-one coaching, the personal touch is the product.

Our story

Built by a client who could feel his coach drowning in spreadsheets.

I was Finlay's client before I built anything. Finlay is a great coach, but he ran everything in Google Sheets: sheet by sheet, client by client, checking every set's weight and RPE by hand. He'd tried the coaching apps and left them: too much AI, too little of him. Some weeks I could feel he wasn't quite on top of me. Not because he didn't care. Because one person reading spreadsheets can't keep up with every client.

One day we got talking about it. He said he was looking for "a CRM situation". I said, let me try and build it for you.

NorCoach is what we built, together, with his clients using it every day.

Alex | founder

Questions

What coaches ask us.

My clients won't download another app.

It replaces the sheet, the WhatsApp thread, the Drive folder and the food tracker for them. One app on iPhone or Android, and logging a set takes a few taps.

Spreadsheets are free.

They are, until a client leaves because they felt unseen. One client kept a year longer pays for NorCoach many times over. And a spreadsheet can't tell you who needs you.

Everyone has AI now. Isn't no AI behind the times?

Your clients pay for you, not a model. NorCoach does the arithmetic (bests, trends, next week's weights) and leaves the coaching to you. That's the point of one-on-one.

I've switched apps before. Moving everyone was a nightmare.

That's why we move your clients in with you, as a founding coach.

What if it breaks mid-workout?

Every set saves the moment it's entered, one by one. Nothing waits for the end of the session.

Will anyone answer if something's wrong?

Yes. You write to us inside NorCoach and a person who builds it answers.

Do my clients need MyFitnessPal?

No. NorCoach has its own food diary, with everyday foods by the gram, at no extra cost.

What does it cost?

Founding coaches start with a 14-day free trial, then a founding price that never goes up for them. No paid add-ons. We'll share the price on your call.

Is my clients' data safe?

Your clients' data is yours: only you and your clients see it, and you can export all of it whenever you like. Payments go through Stripe and card details are never stored by us.

Your clients should never wonder if you're looking.

Founding coaches get their clients moved in with them and a direct line to the people who build it.
