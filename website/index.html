The problem

Clients rarely quit over a bad programme. They quit when they feel their coach has stopped paying attention.

With a few clients, you see every set and answer every message the same day. With twenty or thirty, things start to slip: a check-in reply goes out a day late, then two, and a stalled lift goes unnoticed for weeks. You care just as much as before. But your clients feel the difference, and that's when they start looking elsewhere. Keeping one client a year longer at €180 a month is €2,160 you don't have to win back.

"My coach never really checked in, other than to give a 'thumb's up'."A client, on their online coach

"Previously, it felt much more personal and tailored."A client who went back to a former coach, then left again

Our story

Built by a client who could feel his coach drowning in spreadsheets.

I was Finlay's client before I built anything. Finlay runs Full Potential Coaching in Amsterdam and he's a great coach, but he ran everything in Google Sheets: sheet by sheet, client by client, checking every set's weight and RPE by hand. He'd tried a few coaching apps and left them: none of them was right for him. Some weeks I could feel he wasn't quite on top of me. Not because he didn't care, but because at some point one person reading spreadsheets can't keep up with every client.

One day we got talking about it. He said he was looking for "a CRM situation", and I offered to build it for him.

Norcoach is what we built, together. His clients use it every day.

Alex | founder

How it works

Each client's whole plan, on one screen.

Every client has a plan made of phases: blocks of weeks with one goal each, like a 6-week strength block or a 12-week lean bulk. You run three side by side, for training, nutrition and lifestyle, and line up the next ones before the current one ends. Your client sees which phase they're in and which week on their home screen. You see, week by week, what they actually did against it.

Lifestyle

This is where you set each client's check-ins: daily, weekly or monthly, on whatever matters for them, like sleep, steps, weight, energy or stress. They fill them in on their phone in a few taps, and you see every answer on a graph.

Training

This is where you build each client's programme, week by week. On their side, they open the session, log every set as they go and send you a video when you ask for one. You see what they did against what you planned, with their bests and strength trends worked out.

Nutrition

This is where you set each client's nutrition targets, for training days and rest days, and their supplements. On their side, they log what they eat in a food diary, save their own meals, and see how close they are to your targets.

What your client feels

Your clients feel you're on top of their coaching.

That's what Norcoach is built for. Four things make the difference.

No more "which exercise?"

When your client has a question, they ask it from the exercise, meal or check-in it's about: "how should I place my feet on this one?" The message comes to you linked to it. One tap and you're looking at that exact exercise with their sets, without searching or asking which one they mean. It works the same way when you write to them.

Form checks on video

Your client films a set and sends it from the exercise. You watch it slowed down, draw lines and arrows on it and talk them through it, like you're standing next to them. They get the video back on that exercise.

Goals that are theirs

After each call, up to three goals you agreed sit at the top of their app until the next one. Add a weight goal with a target and a date, and they can see whether they're on track.

Notes where they'll read them

Put a note on their training block, a single session, an exercise ("pause at the bottom") or a meal. They read it at the moment it applies, not buried in a chat.

Also in Norcoach: check-ins with graphs, progress photos side by side, calls with notes, invoices and recurring payments, your logo behind every screen, and an app on your phone for chats, video reviews and your calendar.

What we will never build

No AI between you and your clients.

Nothing writes to your clients in your name.

Other apps now offer to draft your replies for you, in your voice. It's quicker, but your client is paying to hear from you, and some can tell. One wrote that the feedback felt automated and AI-generated, rather than coming from a coach who genuinely knew my journey, then left.

The sums are ours. The coaching is yours.

Norcoach works out bests, trends and next week's targets from what your client logged. What to do about it is your call.

Use AI for your own admin if you like. Everything your client reads comes from you.

Questions

What coaches ask us.

Do my clients need to download anything?

Just Norcoach on their phone. It replaces the spreadsheet, the WhatsApp thread, the Drive folder and the food tracker, so they end up with fewer apps, not more.

How do I move my clients over?

You add each client yourself in a few minutes, and short guides show you how. As a founding coach, we set your account up with you on a call and add your first clients together.

What if the app crashes during a workout?

Nothing is lost. Every set saves the moment it is entered, one by one, so nothing waits for the end of the session.

What if there is no signal in the gym?

Your client keeps logging. Their sets stay on the phone and are sent as soon as the signal is back, even if they close the app in between.

Do my clients need a separate food app?

No, they don't need one. Norcoach has an extensive food library built in for your clients to use.

How do I get support?

Write to us from Support in Norcoach's settings, or report a problem with a screenshot. The people who build Norcoach read it and answer you themselves.

How is it different from other coaching apps?

It is built around you, not around AI. Norcoach shows you who needs you and opens the actual video, note or check-in, ready for your reply. Everything your client reads comes from you.

What does it cost?

A 14-day free trial, then a monthly price we'll tell you on a short intro call, before you pay anything. Founding coaches keep that price for good, and nothing is a paid add-on.

Is my clients' data safe?

Your clients' data is yours: only you and your clients see it, and you can export all of it whenever you like. Payments go through Stripe and card details are never stored by us.
