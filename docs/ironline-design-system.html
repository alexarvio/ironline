How the coaching app looks, for anyone designing a new screen. The client side is a phone app; the coach side is a desktop workstation. Both share one palette and one typeface.

Destructive is outlined; filled only as the confirm inside a dialog.
Segmented control · tint track, white active segment, navy text. One treatment.
Training dayRest day
Switch · the standard pill.
Pills · radius 999, 11/800, +0.06em. Category tags · radius 6, same type.
Week 310 / 10 setsDraftTraining
Inputs · white, 1px Border, radius 10. Focus turns the border Accent, no outline, no shift. Phone inputs are 16px and at least 44px tall, or iOS zooms.
Rules of the road
  • Tap targets are 44px; a smaller control grows an invisible hit area.
  • Inline-editable cells are transparent at rest, bordered on hover, white on focus, and save on blur.
  • Coach edits queue on the pending bar. No per-row save buttons beside it.
  • A confirm dialog before anything that moves dates, deletes, or changes what a client sees while a phase is live.

9

Words

  • The client never reads a coach word: no "deploy", no "phase id", no "client 5".
  • A control says what happens; the message after it says what happened.
  • Errors say what went wrong and what to do about it.
  • Coach names in the client app are the display name Finlay set ("Finlay Chedd"), first name in running copy.
