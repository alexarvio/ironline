# NorCoach coach admin · design rules

Read this before touching any coach (admin) screen or pop-up. It is the admin half of the design rules; the client app's are in `docs/DESIGN-RULES.md`. If a request or spec conflicts with this file, follow the file and say so. When Alex settles something new, add it here in the same change.

## 1. Voice: facts, no explanations
- No sentences explaining what a feature is or how it works. No hint lines under fields, no footer hints ("Only you see a draft until it is sent."), no "optional" tags, no info banners.
- At most one quiet fact line under a title: `3 weeks | 9/11 – 29/11`, `Bar Tricep Pushdown | Upper — push`. A tab without one keeps no empty room for it (21 Oct, Alex): `trn-head bare` on Home, Shape update, Meetings and Invoices, `rd-head bare` on Plan.
- Two facts on one line are parted by a short drawn upright bar, never a middle dot (21 Oct, Alex): `Sep` / `SepJoin` (`app/admin/redesign/Sep.tsx`, `.rd-sep` in training/draft.css), which drops empty parts so no bar is left over. A typed "|" is too faint. Tooltips, aria labels, placeholders, toasts and saved text keep plain words.
- The photo sheets are called **Shape update** wherever people read it, admin and client (21 Oct, Alex; was Progress pictures): the tab, the client's screen, notifications and pushes; in a sentence "your shape update", "shape updates". The code keeps its pictures / photos names.
- A real problem is a short fact plus its fix button: `Overlaps Health, 19 Oct – 15 Nov.` [Start Mon 16 Nov].
- Buttons say the action, one word when it can: Save, Delete, Remove, Add, Copy, Schedule it. Not "Save new dates", not "Add as copy of Week 1".
- Empty states are one or two words: `None yet`, `No gyms yet`.
- Say a thing once (20 Oct): the week rail dates every week, so the week's own line doesn't (`0 of 5 sessions done`, no date).
- Don't offer an action that would change nothing (20 Oct): no "Copy week 3 here" when the weeks already match, no copy from an empty week, no "Copy to weeks 5–6" when they're the same.

## 2. Tokens
- Page `#F5F6F8` · surface `#fff` · ink `#141a24` · body `#3a4250` · secondary `#5b6474` · muted `#8b93a1` · faint `#98A2B3`.
- Hairline `#E1E5EB` · button hairline `#DCE1E8` · divider `#EEF0F3`.
- Success green `#2f7a3f` · danger red `#b42318`.
- Archivo, weights 500 and 600 only. Small caps labels: 10.5px, 600, letter-spacing .16em, uppercase, `#5b6474`.
- **Never navy** (`#1d3466`, `#1e3a6e`, `#2f5d8f`) for an action, a selection or a toggle. Actions and selections are ink `#141a24`.

## 3. Track and state colours (one source: `app/admin/phaseChrome.ts`)
- Track accents, `TRACK_ACCENT`: training purple `#3a3390`, nutrition teal `#1d7a70`, lifestyle beige `#a08a5c` (tint `#efede6`).
- How a track colour appears: a tinted tag (background `rgba(accent, .12)`, text and icon in the accent), a tile's icon, the new value in a change, a calendar range (band `rgba(accent, .10)`, first and last day solid). Never a big filled block.
- **A state tag is always colour-coded** (20 Oct, Alex), wherever a phase's or programme's state shows: the Training header, the programme switcher, the Plan and Phases views, every pop-up. **Live green** `#2f7a3f` on `rgba(47,122,63,.12)`, on every track (never the track's own colour); **Scheduled blue** `#1e3a6e` on `#e6ecf3`; **Draft peach** `#b3471d` on `#fbe9e0`; **Past grey** `#5b6474` on `#EEF0F3`. One source: `STATE_TAG` / `stateTagStyle()` in `phaseChrome.ts`. The same when the tag is a menu. Never a black-and-white capsule. (The Scheduled blue is a state, so the no-navy rule in §2 doesn't apply to it.)
- A phase's pop-ups show both tags under the title: `[icon Training] [LIVE]`.
- Lifestyle is one beige everywhere, admin and client (21 Oct): `#a08a5c` for marks and text, `#efede6` behind. Never amber, orange or grey for lifestyle. (The Lifestyle tab's metric groups have their own colours in `app/lib/categoryColors.ts`; those are groups of metrics, not the track.)

## 4. Pop-ups: one pattern
- Opening or closing a pop-up never moves the page (21 Oct, Alex): the admin keeps its scrollbar, and the scroll lock's stand-in margin is cancelled in app/globals.css. Don't add a lock of your own.
- A field in a `.dlh` card is `.dlh-line` (a clean line) under a `.dlh-label`.
- Use the `.dlh` card (`training/trn.css`) or, for pop-ups with a calendar, `.npd` (`plan/phase-dialog.css`). Use the existing components before writing a new one: `ConfirmDialog`, `RemoveDialog`, `DeletePhaseDialog`, `DeleteSessionDialog` (training), `MoveDialog`, `PhaseDialog`, `ScheduleDialog`.
- Card: white, radius 20, padding 20/20/16, shadow `0 30px 60px -24px rgba(16,24,40,.35)` plus a 1px `#E1E5EB` ring, no border. No gradients, glows, washes, big header icons, coloured header bands or chips in navy/indigo fills.
- Head: title 18px/600 and a round ghost ✕ (28px, `#8b93a1`). Tags or one fact line under it.
- Footer: right-aligned, no line above it. Cancel is a text button (`#5b6474`). The action is a black pill (36px, radius 999). A destructive action is red words, no pill. Delete of an existing thing sits on the left.
- Destructive confirms: `Delete X?` + `This permanently deletes the phase. It can't be undone.` (or the equivalent) + red Delete. When the client's data would go (logged sets, trained sessions), add a tick that must be checked first. Removing a row asks first; nothing destructive happens on one click.
- Text inputs are a clean line: no box, `border-bottom: 1px #E1E5EB`, ink on focus, 15px/500.
- A note that saves (Note to client, the goals/note pop-over): no line until something is typed; then the line appears as a separator and Cancel / Save show.
- Phase goals: three bullet lines (a dot and a clean line each), folded behind a chevron row with the count. Never a textarea.

## 5. Choices and toggles
- Two or three short options: a capsule (36px, white, hairline `#DCE1E8`, 4px of air between the chosen one and the edge, 13px words), the chosen one ink with white text. KG/LBS, the time window, the phase switcher's Live/Scheduled/Draft/Past. One rule for every tab (21 Oct, Alex): `.rd-btn-group` in `measurements/measurements.css`; no per-tab copies.
- The one exception (20 Oct, Alex): the client tabs (Home, Plan, Training…) are a grey track with the tab on screen a white pill, its words in ink (never blue).
- A choice that is the action itself is a menu, not a capsule and a button (20 Oct, Alex): + Week opens Blank / Copy Week 6 / Copy Week 3, and a pick adds the week.
- Picking a category, track or type with icons: tiles across the whole row, equal widths, white with a hairline, the icon in its colour, the chosen tile filled ink. Event categories and the phase Track picker.
- A status that has actions (a Draft or Scheduled programme) is itself the menu: click the tag for Schedule it / Make it live / Back to draft. No separate navy buttons for them.
- Date boxes (Starts / Ends): rounded boxes, radius 12, hairline, ringed in ink while armed. Not capsules.

## 6. Dropdowns and search
- A list floats over the page (`Popover` / absolute). It never pushes the page down or stretches a card.
- Six rows, then it scrolls inside. A Create / Use row stays pinned at its foot, outside the scroll.
- Create your own (an exercise, a metric, a supplement) opens in that same floating panel (21 Oct, Alex): a small caps label, the name and its group on clean lines, Cancel in words and an ink Add. The card under it never stretches, and no line explains where it lands.
- The list is as wide as its field, and a search field is only as wide as its list (560px max), not the whole row.
- Never the browser's own `<datalist>` or `<select>` list. Use `Picker` or the floating list; a plain `+ Add` that opens a menu when the choices are fixed.

## 7. Dates
- A date in words is day/month numerals (20 Oct, Alex): `Scheduled · starts 9/11`, `5/10 – 2/11`, `Start 16/11`. Never `9 Nov` or `Mon 9 Nov` in a pill, a fact line, a note, a button or a toast; the year only when it isn't this one (`9/11/2027`). Grids keep their month names: the timeline's axis (`9 Nov` under `NOV`), the week rail, calendars. One helper writes them, server and screen alike: `dayMonth` (`app/lib/dayMonth.ts`); no local copies.
- Every date field, everywhere (20 Oct, Alex): typed or picked, always both. `DateText`: digits only, slashes fill in (0811 → 08/11/), dd/mm/yyyy, a pasted 2026-11-08 is turned round. A single date is `DatePick` (the typed field with the calendar on its icon); a range is two `DateText` boxes beside `MonthRange`. Never `<input type="date">`, never a calendar-only picker, never a date shown as words where it can be set.
- The month calendar beside the dates (`MonthRange`: phase, event, schedule pop-ups) steps a month with its arrows and a week per wheel or trackpad scroll (21 Oct, Alex); the month named is the one the six weeks mostly show, and picking a day doesn't move them.
- One calendar (21 Oct, Alex): every month a coach picks on is `MonthRange` (`app/admin/redesign/MonthRange.tsx`, styles `month-range.css`): the phase pop-up's, the event pop-up's, Dates / Schedule, every `DatePick` (one day: from = to), the meeting scheduler. Six weeks; ‹ › step a month, the wheel or trackpad steps a week. Ink and grey; a colour only where it marks a category (the phase pop-up's track, the event's kind, via `--npd-c`). Never a month grid of its own.
- The format lives in `DateText` alone, so a coach setting (dd/mm/yyyy or yyyy-mm-dd, to come) switches every field at once. Don't format a typed date anywhere else.
- Phase dates: no earlier than a year back (`min`). Every phase runs in whole Monday-to-Sunday weeks, Lifestyle and Nutrition too (21 Oct, Alex); the dates snap, and no sentence explains it. Make it live now starts a phase this week's Monday.

## 8. Lists, rows and tables
- Training sessions: separate white cards (radius 18, hairline, 8px apart), no numbers, the whole row opens it, ⋯ shows on hover only.
- A cardio row is a row like an exercise's (21 Oct, Alex): + Cardio is the search alone; the pick lands in the cardio table with its time ready to type. Time, Pace, Incline and Distance sit under the exercise figure columns (Sets, kg, Reps, the fourth), the note under Note, each a clean line typed in place with no sample figures; a bare number gets its unit on save ("20" → "20 min"), anything else stays as typed. The name opens Rename only.
- An icon beside words in a button or pill is lifted half a pixel (`translate: 0 -0.5px`) to meet Archivo's capitals (21 Oct, Alex); measured, it sat 0.6px low.
- Row actions in general show on hover only.
- A setting that changes how the week's sessions read sits on the week's line, beside them, not in the programme's ··· menu (21 Oct, Alex): KG/LBS and Columns. Everything on that line is 36px.
- Table heads: white, small caps, no line between a row's head and its column heads, hairlines between rows.
- Status pills: hairline capsules. Completed green, In progress ink, Not started grey, and Not started only on the current week and past weeks, never on weeks still to come.
- One state pill everywhere (21 Oct, Alex): white behind, a 1px edge, 24px, 11.5px/600. Complete / Completed / Done green (edge at 35%), open or due rust, urgent red (`trn-state bad`), live or to read ink, missing / missed / not started grey (`#E1E5EB` edge, `#8b93a1` words). Never a filled green or tinted block (`trn-state`, `rls-vs`, `rt-state` all wear it). Down a column every pill is the column's width, the word centred.
- A summary lives in the column head (`ENJOYMENT  AVG 8`), never as a fake row of data. The Lifestyle check-ins table too (21 Oct, Alex): each metric's head says `CHANGE +1.2 (+2%)` (weight, body fat, measurements: the unit kg, %, cm decides) or `AVG 7.4` (sleep, resting HR, steps, water, /10 scales); the coach switches it in the metric's ··· menu.
- A change from → to: two columns, the old value struck through in grey, the new one in the track's accent. No arrows.
- NOW on the week rail: the word in black with a green pulse on its left. No pill. Only on the week this calendar week really is (21 Oct, Alex): an ended programme, or one whose weeks have run out, has no NOW (`programWeekNow`), every week over.
- A video on an exercise shows on its row and its session's row in every state (21 Oct, Alex: an ask had no icon): asked, a quiet grey ring (waiting for the client); in, rust (waiting for the coach); replied, green. Ask for a video names the exercise alone under its title.
- NOW on a timeline (Plan, Events): an ink line with a black dot on top that pulses (20 Oct, Alex).
- The tracks always run Lifestyle, Training, Nutrition (20 Oct, Alex): the Plan's lanes, the track tiles, the client tabs.
- Use the width (20 Oct, Alex): a section with little in it is one row: its title, its items and add on the left, its summary and fold on the right (the After-workout check). Never a title line, a pill line and a fold line stacked in a wide card.
- A log (the Events log, 20 Oct, Alex): one line a row; a note runs on right after the title in grey (21 Oct), never under it. It keeps only what is over (21 Oct), the latest ended first, with no filter; what is on or coming lives on the grid above.
- A row that opens has its chevron right after its title (and its small fact), pointing right, turned down when open, as Training's sessions (21 Oct, Alex): `Fold` (app/admin/redesign/Fold.tsx, `.rd-fold`). Never in a column of its own on the left. The Events log, Lifestyle check-ins, the Nutrition log, Meetings past calls and All sheets all wear it. A row with nothing in it (a check-in with nothing submitted) has no chevron and doesn't open (21 Oct, Alex). A client's note on a log's row is the chat icon after the date and chevron (`.rd-notemark`), its words only once the row is opened, with Reply there (Lifestyle check-ins, the Nutrition log; 21 Oct, Alex), so every row keeps one height.
- A feed's or a log's date (21 Oct, Alex): every row of a log starts with its date as `Tue, 21 Oct` (the weekday, a comma, the day, the short month; the year only when it isn't this one), then the name of the thing: the session, the sheet (`Week 3`), the call's title, the event's title. A row that opens has its chevron right after the date; a range keeps the faint arrow (`Tue, 21 Oct → Fri, 24 Oct`). A weekly check-in is dated by its Monday, a monthly one keeps its month. Home's Activity puts the time after it, faint. Pills, fact lines, buttons and toasts keep `dayMonth` (9/11); group headings (Today, Yesterday) keep their words.
- A client's log (21 Oct, Alex): a time after the date is a column of its own right after the date column, centred in it, so every row's time lines up (`.rd-logtime`; the date column `logDateWidth` of the whole list's dates, not a page's). - Pills down a column (a category, a kind) are all one width, the word centred, whatever its length.
- Two dates in a row (`14/10 → 5/11`) get air: a faint grey arrow with room either side.
- A bar on a timeline is outlined in the colour it is now (its track or kind at half strength); a draft's outline is dashed.
- The chat panel (21 Oct, Alex): on a client's screens it opens on their thread with `‹ All chats` over it (a rust count when someone else wrote); All chats is every client's chat, newest first, in one white card (`ChatList`: name 600, last line grey, the time today or `9/11`, a rust dot unread, the page's client ringed; a photo, video, voice message or file as its icon then the words sent with it, or with none `Photo`, `Video`, `Voice message`, `File` (22 Oct, Alex), one `ChatLastLine` for the laptop and the phone), and a row opens that thread in the panel without the page moving. - The client's phone (21 Oct, Alex: so the coach is not blind): a second tab under the chat's, white with a hairline, opens the same panel on the client's app as they see it (`PhonePreview`, `/client?phone=1`): the real app, read-only. ## 9. Saving
- The Training, Lifestyle, Nutrition and Plan tabs autosave (Lifestyle and Nutrition since 21 Oct; Shape update keeps the status for Remind, its Sheet setup is Cancel / Save in a pop-up): one status per tab, right after the title and its state tag (21 Oct, Alex: title, chevron, state, status), 13px (`✓ All changes saved` / `Saving…` / `Not saved · Retry`), none per section or row. Nothing until the first save on that screen (22 Oct, Alex: it said saved on a client nobody had touched): `useSaidSaved` (useSaveQueue.ts), on every tab with the status, the Calendar too. A change shows at once; typed figures go a moment after the typing stops; a row just added is faded until its save comes back, in the place the saved list will have it (a new supplement goes once it has a name and the row is left). Other tabs still use the pending bar; ask Alex before switching another tab to autosave. The queue is `useSaveQueue` (app/admin/redesign/useSaveQueue.ts).
- Nothing on the client's screen changes before the coach (or client) confirms: a sheet with Done applies on Done.
- A name typed in place shows the moment Enter is pressed, never the old one while it saves (21 Oct, Alex).
