# NorCoach coach admin · design rules

Read this before touching any coach (admin) screen or pop-up. It is the admin half of the design rules; the client app's are in `docs/DESIGN-RULES.md`. If a request or spec conflicts with this file, follow the file and say so. When Alex settles something new, add it here in the same change.

## 1. Voice: facts, no explanations
- No sentences explaining what a feature is or how it works. No hint lines under fields, no footer hints ("Only you see a draft until it is sent."), no "optional" tags, no info banners.
- At most one quiet fact line under a title: `3 weeks | 9/11 – 29/11`, `Bar Tricep Pushdown | Upper — push`. A tab without one keeps no empty room for it (21 Oct, Alex): `trn-head bare` on Home, Shape update, Meetings and Invoices, `rd-head bare` on Plan.
- Two facts on one line are parted by a short drawn upright bar, never a middle dot (21 Oct, Alex): `Sep` / `SepJoin` (`app/admin/redesign/Sep.tsx`, `.rd-sep` in training/draft.css), which drops empty parts so no bar is left over. A typed "|" is too faint. Tooltips, aria labels, placeholders, toasts and saved text keep plain words.
- The photo sheets are called **Shape update** wherever people read it, admin and client (21 Oct, Alex; was Progress pictures): the tab, the client's screen, notifications and pushes; in a sentence "your shape update", "shape updates". The code keeps its pictures / photos names.
- A real problem is a short fact plus its fix button: `Overlaps Health, 19 Oct – 15 Nov.` [Start Mon 16 Nov].
- Buttons say the action, one word when it can: Save, Delete, Remove, Add, Copy, Schedule it. Not "Save new dates", not "Add as copy of Week 1".
- Empty states are one or two words: `None yet`, `No gyms yet`.
- Say a thing once (20 Oct): the week rail dates every week, so the week's own line doesn't (`0 of 5 sessions done`, no date).
- Every section answers one question (22 Oct, Alex), and a screen holds only sections that belong to its question. The Feed: what do I owe (Needs you) and what happened (Activity). How each client is doing is a status board, not the Feed's question. Before adding a section, name its question; if the screen already answers a different one, it goes elsewhere.
- Don't offer an action that would change nothing (20 Oct): no "Copy week 3 here" when the weeks already match, no copy from an empty week, no "Copy to weeks 5–6" when they're the same. Carrying a week's changes forward is **Apply to the rest** (23 Oct, Alex; was "Copy to weeks 4–6"), only while applying would change something (23 Oct, Alex: "once I apply and there are no other changes, the button disappears"; the same compare as its list, so logged sessions kept and weights the progression raises again never keep it there), the weeks it reaches in its tooltip. Its confirm, and a session's own Apply to remaining weeks, is its title and the changes alone, no sentence over them (23 Oct, Alex: "just show me quickly what has been changed"), and lists what will change (23 Oct, Alex: "naming nicely and structured the changes"; `ApplyChangesDialog`, `applyPreview.ts`): by session, a row per exercise or cardio that changes, each figure its old value struck grey and the new in training purple, parted by Sep; Added green, Removed struck, New order and a rename on the session, a video ask that goes, logged sets that go in rust; grouped under the weeks they reach (`19/10 → 1/11`) when the later weeks change differently; only what the action really changes (weights in the coach's unit, a weight the progression would raise again left out), the list scrolling inside the card.

## 2. Tokens
- Page `#F5F6F8` · surface `#fff` · ink `#141a24` · body `#3a4250` · secondary `#5b6474` · muted `#8b93a1` · faint `#98A2B3`.
- Hairline `#E1E5EB` · button hairline `#DCE1E8` · divider `#EEF0F3`.
- Success green `#2f7a3f` · danger red `#b42318`.
- Archivo, weights 500 and 600 only. Small caps labels: 10.5px, 600, letter-spacing .16em, uppercase, `#5b6474`.
- **Never navy** (`#1d3466`, `#1e3a6e`, `#2f5d8f`) for an action, a selection or a toggle. Actions and selections are ink `#141a24`.

## 3. Track and state colours (one source: `app/admin/phaseChrome.ts`)
- Track accents, `TRACK_ACCENT`: training purple `#3a3390`, nutrition teal `#1d7a70`, lifestyle beige `#a08a5c` (tint `#efede6`).
- How a track colour appears: a tinted tag (background `rgba(accent, .12)`, text and icon in the accent), a tile's icon, the new value in a change, a calendar range (band `rgba(accent, .10)`, first and last day solid). Never a big filled block.
- **A state tag is always colour-coded** (20 Oct, Alex), wherever a phase's or programme's state shows: the Training header, the programme switcher, the Plan and Phases views, every pop-up. **Live green** `#2f7a3f` on `rgba(47,122,63,.12)`, on every track (never the track's own colour); **Scheduled blue** `#1e3a6e` on `#e6ecf3`; **Draft peach** `#b3471d` on `#fbe9e0`; **Past grey** `#5b6474` on `#EEF0F3`. One source: `STATE_TAG` / `stateTagStyle()` in `phaseChrome.ts`. The same when the tag is a menu. Never a black-and-white capsule. (The Scheduled blue is a state, so the no-navy rule in §2 doesn't apply to it.)
- A phase's pop-ups show both tags under the title: `[icon Training] [LIVE]`.
- Lifestyle is one beige everywhere, admin and client (21 Oct): `#a08a5c` for marks and text, `#efede6` behind. Never amber, orange or grey for lifestyle. (The Lifestyle tab's metric groups have their own colours in `app/lib/categoryColors.ts`; those are groups of metrics, not the track.)

## 4. Pop-ups: one pattern
- Opening or closing a pop-up never moves the page (21 Oct, Alex): the admin keeps its scrollbar, and the scroll lock's stand-in margin is cancelled in app/globals.css. Don't add a lock of your own.
- A pop-up with tabs keeps one height across them, so switching never moves it (23 Oct, Alex): the demo video's Paste a link | Upload a file | Your videos share one panel height, the drop zone filling it, the list scrolling in it.
- A field in a `.dlh` card is `.dlh-line` (a clean line) under a `.dlh-label`.
- Use the `.dlh` card (`training/trn.css`) or, for pop-ups with a calendar, `.npd` (`plan/phase-dialog.css`). Use the existing components before writing a new one: `ConfirmDialog`, `RemoveDialog`, `DeletePhaseDialog`, `DeleteSessionDialog` (training), `MoveDialog`, `PhaseDialog`, `ScheduleDialog`.
- Card: white, radius 20, padding 20/20/16, shadow `0 30px 60px -24px rgba(16,24,40,.35)` plus a 1px `#E1E5EB` ring, no border. No gradients, glows, washes, big header icons, coloured header bands or chips in navy/indigo fills.
- Head: title 18px/600 and a round ghost ✕ (28px, `#8b93a1`). Tags or one fact line under it.
- Footer: right-aligned, no line above it. Cancel is a text button (`#5b6474`). The action is a black pill (36px, radius 999). A destructive action is red words, no pill. Delete of an existing thing sits on the left.
- Destructive confirms: `Delete X?` + `This permanently deletes the phase. It can't be undone.` (or the equivalent) + red Delete. When the client's data would go (logged sets, trained sessions), add a tick that must be checked first. Removing a row asks first; nothing destructive happens on one click.
- Text inputs are a clean line: no box, `border-bottom: 1px #E1E5EB`, ink on focus, 15px/500.
- A note that saves (Note to client, the note pop-over; its goals half off since 23 Oct): no line until something is typed; then the line appears as a separator and Cancel / Save show.
- Phase goals (off since 23 Oct, Alex: To focus on replaces them; `FEATURES.phaseGoals` in app/lib/features.ts, the stored goals kept and nothing writing them): three bullet lines (a dot and a clean line each), folded behind a chevron row with the count. Never a textarea.

## 5. Choices and toggles
- Two or three short options: a capsule (36px, white, hairline `#DCE1E8`, 4px of air between the chosen one and the edge, 13px words), the chosen one ink with white text. KG/LBS, the time window, the phase switcher's Live/Scheduled/Draft/Past. One rule for every tab (21 Oct, Alex): `.rd-btn-group` in `measurements/measurements.css`; no per-tab copies.
- The one exception (20 Oct, Alex): the client tabs (Home, Plan, Training…) are a grey track with the tab on screen a white pill, its words in ink (never blue). And Home's Muscle balance windows (23 Oct, Alex): a window on is outlined and worded in its line's colour, empty inside.
- A choice that is the action itself is a menu, not a capsule and a button (20 Oct, Alex): + Week opens Blank / Copy Week 6 / Copy Week 3, and a pick adds the week.
- Picking a category, track or type with icons: tiles across the whole row, equal widths, white with a hairline, the icon in its colour, the chosen tile filled ink. Event categories and the phase Track picker.
- A status that has actions (a Draft or Scheduled programme) is itself the menu: click the tag for Schedule it / Make it live / Back to draft. No separate navy buttons for them.
- Date boxes (Starts / Ends): rounded boxes, radius 12, hairline, ringed in ink while armed. Not capsules.

## 6. Dropdowns and search
- A list floats over the page (`Popover` / absolute). It never pushes the page down or stretches a card.
- Six rows, then it scrolls inside. A Create / Use row stays pinned at its foot, outside the scroll.
- Create your own (an exercise, a metric, a supplement) opens in that same floating panel (21 Oct, Alex): a small caps label, the name and its group on clean lines, Cancel in words and an ink Add. The card under it never stretches, and no line explains where it lands.
- The list is as wide as its field, and a search field is only as wide as its list (560px max), not the whole row.
- Never the browser's own `<datalist>` or `<select>` list. Use `Picker` or the floating list; a plain `+ Add` that opens a menu when the choices are fixed.

## 7. Dates
- A date in words is day/month numerals (20 Oct, Alex): `Scheduled · starts 9/11`, `5/10 – 2/11`, `Start 16/11`. Never `9 Nov` or `Mon 9 Nov` in a pill, a fact line, a note, a button or a toast; the year only when it isn't this one (`9/11/2027`). Grids keep their month names: the timeline's axis (`9 Nov` under `NOV`), the week rail, calendars. One helper writes them, server and screen alike: `dayMonth` (`app/lib/dayMonth.ts`); no local copies.
- Every date field, everywhere (20 Oct, Alex): typed or picked, always both. `DateText`: digits only, slashes fill in (0811 → 08/11/), dd/mm/yyyy, a pasted 2026-11-08 is turned round. A single date is `DatePick` (the typed field with the calendar on its icon); a range is two `DateText` boxes beside `MonthRange`. Never `<input type="date">`, never a calendar-only picker, never a date shown as words where it can be set.
- The month calendar beside the dates (`MonthRange`: phase, event, schedule pop-ups) steps a month with its arrows and a week per wheel or trackpad scroll (21 Oct, Alex); the month named is the one the six weeks mostly show, and picking a day doesn't move them.
- One calendar (21 Oct, Alex): every month a coach picks on is `MonthRange` (`app/admin/redesign/MonthRange.tsx`, styles `month-range.css`): the phase pop-up's, the event pop-up's, Dates / Schedule, every `DatePick` (one day: from = to), the meeting scheduler. Six weeks; ‹ › step a month, the wheel or trackpad steps a week. Ink and grey; a colour only where it marks a category (the phase pop-up's track, the event's kind, via `--npd-c`). Never a month grid of its own.
- The format lives in `DateText` alone, so a coach setting (dd/mm/yyyy or yyyy-mm-dd, to come) switches every field at once. Don't format a typed date anywhere else.
- Phase dates: no earlier than a year back (`min`). Every phase runs in whole Monday-to-Sunday weeks, Lifestyle and Nutrition too (21 Oct, Alex); the dates snap, and no sentence explains it. Make it live now starts a phase this week's Monday.

## 8. Lists, rows and tables
- Training sessions: separate white cards (radius 18, hairline, 8px apart), no numbers, the whole row opens it, ⋯ shows on hover only.
- A done week is look-only (23 Oct, Alex): a week whose dates are over, and every week of an ended programme. Everything in it can be read (sessions open, what the client did, their answers, notes and videos, with Reply and the recorder, Message, KG/LBS, Columns); nothing that changes the plan is offered: no adding, removing, reordering, renaming or copying, no Apply to the rest, no editing a waiting video ask, its figures and notes words, not fields (`weekLocked`, `Fixed`). No + Week once every week is over. An empty done week says `None`. An ended programme keeps no blanket fade and no "Ended…" note: its name is words, Note to {First} read only (gone when empty; Phase goals off since 23 Oct), no Edit dates; New programme, Client gyms and Delete phase stay.
- Rogue sessions (the client's own workouts off the plan) are a section of their own under the week's (23 Oct, Alex): one white card as the After-workout check, `Rogue sessions` and the count in its head, a session a row. A week with none has no section at all, no `None this week`.
- A cardio row is a row like an exercise's (21 Oct, Alex): + Cardio is the search alone; the pick lands in the cardio table with its time ready to type. Time, Pace, Incline and Distance sit under the exercise figure columns (Sets, kg, Reps, the fourth), the note under Note, each a clean line typed in place with no sample figures; a bare number gets its unit on save ("20" → "20 min"), anything else stays as typed. The name opens Rename only.
- An icon beside words in a button or pill is lifted half a pixel (`translate: 0 -0.5px`) to meet Archivo's capitals (21 Oct, Alex); measured, it sat 0.6px low.
- Row actions in general show on hover only.
- A setting that changes how the week's sessions read sits on the week's line, beside them, not in the programme's ··· menu (21 Oct, Alex): KG/LBS and Columns. Everything on that line is 36px.
- Table heads: white, small caps, no line between a row's head and its column heads, hairlines between rows.
- Status pills: hairline capsules. Completed green, In progress ink, Not started grey, and Not started only on the current week and past weeks, never on weeks still to come.
- One state pill everywhere (21 Oct, Alex): white behind, a 1px edge, 24px, 11.5px/600. Complete / Completed / Done green (edge at 35%), open or due rust, urgent red (`trn-state bad`), live or to read ink, missing / missed / not started grey (`#E1E5EB` edge, `#8b93a1` words). Never a filled green or tinted block (`trn-state`, `rls-vs`, `rt-state` all wear it). Down a column every pill is the column's width, the word centred.
- A summary lives in the column head (`ENJOYMENT  AVG 8`), never as a fake row of data. The Lifestyle check-ins table too (21 Oct, Alex): each metric's head says `CHANGE +1.2 (+2%)` (weight, body fat, measurements: the unit kg, %, cm decides) or `AVG 7.4` (sleep, resting HR, steps, water, /10 scales); the coach switches it in the metric's ··· menu.
- A change from → to: two columns, the old value struck through in grey, the new one in the track's accent. No arrows. Over the new values a small-caps NEW in the same colour (22 Oct, Alex: one rule for every Move pop-up, the Plan's phases and events and the Phases screen); the old ones need no label, the strike says it. A value that stays (the start when only the end moved, the length of a move) shows in both columns, never an empty space: grey and not struck in the old one, ink in New (23 Oct, Alex).
- NOW on the week rail: the word in black with a green pulse on its left. No pill. Only on the week this calendar week really is (21 Oct, Alex): an ended programme, or one whose weeks have run out, has no NOW (`programWeekNow`), every week over.
- Training's 7 days and 30 days cells count by the calendar as Home's Strength does (23 Oct, Alex): the exercise's best estimated one-rep max (Epley, weight × (1 + reps ÷ 30), a day's best set) in the last 7 (30) days against the 7 (30) before, only when done in both, a dash otherwise (`exerciseTrends`). So a row is its share of its muscle group's figure on Home.
- An exercise's progress pop-up (23 Oct, Alex: built from mockup B, `ExerciseProgressDialog`, the `.dlh` card): the name and ✕; one line, the verdict as a state pill (Progressing green, Stalling rust, Dropping red, Too early grey) then the targets and the gym parted by Sep, the gym a floating list when there are two, All gyms first and where it opens (23 Oct, Alex); every session of the exercise the 7 and 30 days cells count, any programme, any gym (`exerciseTrends`' sessions, never only this row's slot); Estimated 1RM now big with its change and the best set grey; 7 days | 30 days | All time as the capsule, each only when it holds other sessions than the next wider, opening on the cell clicked (23 Oct, Alex: "I click on 30 days... it still shows 1 week"), which is always offered; in 7 and 30 days the change is the cell's own figure, `+16.7% on the 30 days before`, All time `since d/m`; stalling, its fact and a Suggest pill that drafts the message (dropping, its fact alone); the line chart in training purple, dates spaced by time, the best ringed, a stall rust; the sessions as a log, the latest 4 then a Fold; the ink Message {First} at its foot. No explanation of how it is worked out.
- An assisted lift (its name or variation says assisted, 23 Oct, Alex) is weighed by the client's body weight (latest reading, else their starting weight) less the help, so less help reads stronger, everywhere a lift is compared: Home's Strength and volume, Training's trends, the progress pop-up, the target that moves on after a set. No body weight known: it is left out of comparisons (the pop-up says `No body weight yet`).
- A video on an exercise shows on its row and its session's row in every state (21 Oct, Alex: an ask had no icon): asked, a quiet grey ring (waiting for the client); in, rust (waiting for the coach); replied, green. Ask for a video names the exercise alone under its title.
- NOW on a timeline (Plan, Events): an ink line with a black dot on top that pulses (20 Oct, Alex).
- The tracks always run Lifestyle, Training, Nutrition (20 Oct, Alex): the Plan's lanes, the track tiles, the client tabs.
- Use the width (20 Oct, Alex): a section with little in it is one row: its title, its items and add on the left, its summary and fold on the right (the After-workout check). Never a title line, a pill line and a fold line stacked in a wide card.

- A row that opens has its chevron right after its title (and its small fact), pointing right, turned down when open, as Training's sessions (21 Oct, Alex): `Fold` (app/admin/redesign/Fold.tsx, `.rd-fold`). Never in a column of its own on the left. The Events log, Lifestyle check-ins, the Nutrition log, Meetings past calls and All sheets all wear it. A row with nothing in it (a check-in with nothing submitted) has no chevron and doesn't open (21 Oct, Alex). A client's note on a log's row is the chat icon after the date and chevron (`.rd-notemark`), its words only once the row is opened, with Reply there (Lifestyle check-ins, the Nutrition log; 21 Oct, Alex), so every row keeps one height. - A feed's or a log's date (21 Oct, Alex): every row of a log starts with its date as `Tue, 21 Oct` (the weekday, a comma, the day, the short month; the year only when it isn't this one), then the name of the thing: the session, the sheet (`Week 3`), the call's title, the event's title. A row that opens has its chevron right after the date; a range keeps the faint arrow (`Tue, 21 Oct → Fri, 24 Oct`). A weekly check-in is dated by its Monday, a monthly one keeps its month. Home's Activity puts the time after it, faint. Pills, fact lines, buttons and toasts keep `dayMonth` (9/11); group headings (Today, Yesterday) keep their words.

## 9. Saving
- The Training, Lifestyle, Nutrition, Plan and Meetings tabs autosave (Lifestyle and Nutrition since 21 Oct, Meetings since 23 Oct, Alex: the words typed on a call; its pop-ups keep their buttons; Shape update keeps the status for Remind, its Sheet setup is Cancel / Save in a pop-up): one status per tab, right after the title and its state tag (21 Oct, Alex: title, chevron, state, status), 13px (`✓ All changes saved` / `Saving…` / `Not saved · Retry`; `Saving…` too while typed words wait to go), none per section or row. Nothing until the first save on that screen (22 Oct, Alex: it said saved on a client nobody had touched): `useSaidSaved` (useSaveQueue.ts), on every tab with the status, the Calendar too. A change shows at once; typed figures and words go a moment after the typing stops (and on leaving the field), and a save coming back never changes a field being typed in; a row just added is faded until its save comes back, in the place the saved list will have it (a new supplement goes once it has a name and the row is left). Other tabs still use the pending bar; ask Alex before switching another tab to autosave. The queue is `useSaveQueue` (app/admin/redesign/useSaveQueue.ts).
- Nothing on the client's screen changes before the coach (or client) confirms: a sheet with Done applies on Done.
- A name typed in place shows the moment Enter is pressed, never the old one while it saves (21 Oct, Alex).
- Every body weight shown is the client's own latest reading, from every place they log it (23 Oct, Alex): each Weight metric, any phase or rhythm, and the old Weight field, one read (`bodyWeightReadings`, queries.ts), in kg; never a copy, never the starting weight while there is a reading. Weight is asked once (23 Oct, Alex): while a Weight metric is asked on any rhythm, the client's Measure form leaves out the old Weight field and nothing counts it due (`measureFieldsAskedOf`); its readings stay and are never copied into the metric.
- The coach's screens read again when the coach comes back to the window (23 Oct, Alex: so what the client logged meanwhile shows without a reload; `RefreshOnReturn`, mounted by the rail): at most once every 15 seconds, nothing shown for it, never while a field has the keyboard, a pop-up, menu or list is open, a button is held, a save is on its way or failed, or changes wait (a pending bar, Settings' Save bar, Training's queue). Anything that holds changes calls `holdRefresh` (`useHoldWhile`, `useHoldRefresh`); a skipped read runs once it clears. A screen arriving at a linked row (`#day-…`, `#sheet-…`) does so on arrival and on a new `#` only, never again on a read.

## 9b. A track tab is built as Training (21 Oct, Alex)
- The header: the phase's name to click and type (Enter or a click away saves, Esc leaves it), ▾ beside it to switch phase, the state tag as the menu of what can be done; one fact line (`4 weeks · 19/10 – 29/11 · week 1 of 6`); on the right Note to {client} as a pill (Phase goals beside it off since 23 Oct), then ⋯ (Message, Edit dates, New phase, Delete phase). No goals row across the page. The phase pop-ups (the Plan's, the Phases screen's, the old admin's) have no Phase goals either, off since 23 Oct.
- A track with no phase (22 Oct, Alex): the track's name is the title (`Lifestyle`, `Training`, `Nutrition`; never `Check-ins`, `Programme` or `Targets`), no eyebrow over it, no room kept for a fact line (`trn-head bare`, as every header without one). The ▾ lists real phases only, so nothing reads live when nothing is (the client-level sheet behind it is not a phase). With nothing set up yet (a metric line with no phase and nothing logged counts as nothing; the untouched Weight line once added by itself is dropped as the coach opens the client, `dropUnusedAutoWeight`) the tab is that header and one hairline card, `No phase yet` and New phase (`NoPhase`; a training phase without a programme, `No programme yet` and New programme, `NoProgramme`); never the old centred card or a navy button.
- Each section's title (20px/600) and its status on the page, its controls on the right; the content in one white card (radius 18, hairline).
- Adding to a list is the exercise search: one bar with an ink edge, the list floating under it (six rows, Create pinned at its foot), the bar staying for the next pick. Lifestyle's metrics have no time of day: every metric is asked all day.
- Lifestyle's tracked metrics are rows (Metric, Group, Last), not pills (21 Oct, Alex tried pills and went back). + Metric under them opens a dropdown with the search bar at its top, so the card never changes while adding.
- A tab's own actions in its header or a section head (Schedule a meeting, Mark completed, Schedule a call) are Training's white pills with an icon (`trn-pill`, as Note to {client}), not black (21 Oct, Alex). A header without a fact line keeps no room for one.
