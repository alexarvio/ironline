# NorCoach · Necessary beliefs (step 4)

The few beliefs a coach must hold before they apply. Built from `01-research.md`, `02-avatar.md` and `03-offer-brief.md`. Per the method: marketing is an argument, not a list of words; every section of the website and every ad leads the coach one step along these, in this order, to the offer. Written 26 Oct 2026.

For each: the belief, what the coach believes today instead, and how we prove it.

---

### 1. "I believe that my clients feeling seen is what keeps them, and keeping them is my whole business."
- **What they believe now:** growth is about getting more clients (content, ads, mentorships); retention is a nice-to-have.
- **Why it must come first:** it turns "software" from an admin cost into the thing that protects their income. Everything else hangs on it.
- **Proof:** the numbers (median client stays 3.2 months; only 45% are still there at a year; attrition is steepest from month 3 to 6); clients' own words ("No one there even knows who I am", "Feels like they say the same thing every week"); the maths (one client kept a year at €180 a month is over €2,000).

### 2. "I believe that past ten or twenty clients I can't see every client properly any more, and that it isn't because I'm a worse coach or not working hard enough."
- **What they believe now:** it's a discipline problem ("I just need better systems", "I need to work Sunday evenings"), or it's fine.
- **Why it matters:** recognition without blame. The coach has to admit the gap without feeling accused, or they defend the spreadsheet.
- **Proof:** "It worked at five clients. It worked at ten." Their own Sunday check-in marathon described back to them; Alex's story as Finlay's client (a good coach who couldn't keep up, through no fault of his own).

### 3. "I believe that the real reason is the scattered signal: the signs a client needs me are spread across sheets, chats and folders, and nothing brings them to me."
- **What they believe now:** the problem is "too much admin" or "the wrong spreadsheet template", so the fix is a tidier sheet or an all-in-one login.
- **Why it matters:** this is the unique mechanism of the problem. Once they see it this way, only a tool that brings the signal to them can fix it.
- **Proof:** walk through one ordinary week: the third week on the same weight (in the sheet), the knee note (in WhatsApp), the form video (in the chat from Tuesday), the missed check-in (in a Google Form that doesn't know who didn't send). Each is visible only if you go looking.

### 4. "I believe that the coaching apps I know put everything behind one login but still make me go looking, so switching to them didn't fix it."
- **What they believe now:** either "apps are all the same" (so why switch again) or "I just need to pick the right big one".
- **Why it matters:** stage 4–5 market. Without this belief, NorCoach is one more "all-in-one" and the coach stays put or picks the market leader.
- **Proof:** their own experience and the reviews (screens, not signals; "Too many button clicks"; "Comments are virtually inaccessible after you leave them"; coach phone apps that don't work); no attacks by name on the page, just the pattern they recognise.

### 5. "I believe that NorCoach brings every sign to me, already worked out, so I can coach forty clients the way I coached five."
- **What they believe now:** "every app claims that."
- **Why it matters:** this is the unique mechanism of the solution, the reason it's different and better.
- **Proof:** show, don't claim. Five short screen recordings: a logged set turning into a PB and a strength trend; the Needs you list clearing itself; a chat link landing on the exact set; Draw & Talk on a real lift; To focus on appearing on the client's home screen. Then Finlay: how he uses it with his clients.

### 6. "I believe that NorCoach is made by people I can reach and trust, and that trying it costs me little: they help me move my clients and my data stays mine."
- **What they believe now:** "Do I trust a new tool from someone I've never met with my clients?" and "switching last time was a nightmare."
- **Why it matters:** trust is the final gate; reviews show support and reliability are what coaches praise most, and new tools are what they fear most.
- **Proof:** the founders by name and face (Alex and Finlay), a real way to message them, sets that save as they're entered, help moving clients for founding coaches [Decision], data export [Decision, build before promising], a founding price kept for life [Decision].

## How the website follows them
1. **Belief 1, opening:** the stakes: clients who feel seen stay.
2. **Belief 2, recognition:** "It worked at ten clients. Then it didn't." Your Sunday, without blame.
3. **Belief 3, the reason:** the scattered signal, one ordinary week walked through.
4. **Belief 4, why switching didn't fix it:** screens, not signals.
5. **Belief 5, the mechanism:** every signal comes to you; five short recordings; Finlay's story.
6. **Belief 6, trust and the offer:** who we are, what we do for founding coaches, apply.

Every ad, post or email should push one of these six, not features.
