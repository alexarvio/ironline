**Conventions**
- `[Belief n]` marks which belief a section moves the reader along. Not shown on the page.
- `[REC n]` is a short silent screen recording (6 to 12 seconds, looping) made from the real app with demo clients; scripts at the end.
- `[Decision]` / `[Proof]` mark what Alex or Finlay must settle or supply before launch. Nothing in brackets ships.
- Words avoided on purpose (spent in this market): all-in-one, save hours, AI-powered, grow your business, scale your business, game-changer, seamless. No competitor named on the page; quotes from public reviews describe what happened, not to whom.
- Words from the market to use: check-ins, programmes, client history, unprofessional, trapped, another number, copy and paste, personal.

## 3. Sound familiar `[Belief 2]`

### It worked at ten clients. Then it didn't.

At five clients you saw everything. Every set, every check-in, every message. You noticed the knee, the stall, the bad week before they told you.

Then you got good at this, and more people wanted you.

Now Sunday evening is check-ins. You open a sheet, scroll to this week, read the weights and the RPE, row by row, then the next client's sheet, then the next. You scroll WhatsApp for the form video from Tuesday. **NEW 7 Nov:** Somewhere past twenty-odd clients, your check-in replies start going out a day late. Then two.

You're not a worse coach than you were at five clients. You're one person, reading spreadsheets.

## 5. Why switching didn't fix it `[Belief 4]`

### Most coaching apps moved your sheets behind one login. The new ones write your replies for you.

Maybe you tried one. Programmes in one place, a client app, a chat. Better than the sheet, at first.

But you still open each client, one by one, check each log and build the picture yourself. More screens, the same Sunday.

**NEW 7 Nov:** And it's your name on it. When the app goes down, your clients don't message the software company. They message you. *"WE are the ones left looking unprofessional,"* as one coach wrote. And still you stay, because years of programmes live inside it. (Ship this only together with the move answer in section 9 and real uptime proof in the FAQ.)

Now the answer on offer is a reply drafted for you, in your voice. It's quicker. But your client is paying to hear from you, and some notice: one wrote that the feedback *"felt automated and AI-generated, rather than coming from a coach who genuinely knew my journey"*, then went elsewhere.

You don't need more screens. You need the signs to come to you, so you can be the coach.

## 6. How Norcoach works `[Belief 5]`

### Every client on a plan you can see.

Coaching runs in phases, on three tracks: Lifestyle, Training and Nutrition. Each phase runs in whole weeks, the next one is lined up before this one ends, and your client sees the phase they're in, and the week, on their home screen. `[Check: the demo's Plan must show phases back to back, or soften "lined up before this one ends"]`

- **Lifestyle:** what they check in on, daily, weekly or monthly: sleep, steps, weight, energy. Every answer on a graph.
- **Training:** the programme, week by week. Every set they log is counted, and next week's weights move on from what they lifted.
- **Nutrition:** targets for training and rest days, supplements, and a food diary they actually use.

(The site's Lifestyle | Training | Nutrition switch: the coach's tab beside the client's screen for each.)

### Every signal comes to you, already worked out.

**Every set, counted.** Clients log each set in a few taps. Their bests, their strength trend on every lift and muscle group, and next week's weights are worked out from what they actually lifted. You read one line, not forty rows. `[REC 2]`

**Needs you.** One list of what you owe, in order: a reply, a video to review, a note to read, an empty week. It clears itself as you go. When it's empty, you're done. Close the laptop. **NEW 7 Nov:** Not a summary: the thing itself, opened where you can answer it. `[REC 1]`

**Links that land.** Every message, notification and row opens the exact thing: the set, the meal, the check-in day, the photo. Not a screen to search. They can point at the squat they mean, and so can you. `[REC 3]`

**Draw & Talk.** They send a lift. You slow it down, draw on it and talk them through it, on the laptop or your phone. The recording goes straight back to them. Like having them in front of you. `[REC 4]`

**To focus on.** After each call, up to three things go to the top of their home screen until the next one. Every day they open the app, they see what you asked of them. `[REC 5]`

**Your face on it.** Wherever you did something in their app, a note on their phase, a message, their goals, it carries your face and your name. They see their coach, not a platform. `[REC 6]`

**And the rest you'd expect, in the same place:** phases on one timeline, check-ins with graphs, a food diary with a clean food list, shape updates with side-by-side compare, calls, chat, invoices and plans, your logo behind every screen, and an app on your phone for chats, videos and your calendar.

## 7. What we will never build `[Belief 4 → 5]`

### No AI between you and your clients.

**Nothing talks to them in your name.** No bot replies, no ghost-written check-in feedback. Every message, review and goal your client gets is yours.

**Your face on everything you did.** A note on their phase, a reply, their three goals, the latest on their home screen: each one wears your face, so they always know a person is behind it.

**The arithmetic is ours. The coaching is yours.** Bests, trends and next week's weights are plain sums from what they logged, shown to you to decide on. Norcoach takes the reading of spreadsheets off you, not the coaching.

**NEW 7 Nov** (replacing the site's "Clients now notice… and they leave", which says the section 5 quote twice and rests on one review): Use whatever you like for your own admin. What your client reads is yours. In one-on-one coaching, the personal touch is the product.

(The site's four sample lines, each with Finlay's face: a note on the training phase, a squat review, a To focus on goal, a message. Caption: *Every line from a person.*)

## 10. FAQ `[Belief 6]`

**My clients won't download another app.** It replaces the sheet, the WhatsApp thread, the Drive folder and the food tracker for them. One app on iPhone or Android, and logging a set takes a few taps. `[Proof: Finlay's clients' adoption]`

**Spreadsheets are free.** They are, until a client leaves because they felt unseen. One client kept a year longer pays for Norcoach many times over. And a spreadsheet can't tell you who needs you.

**NEW 7 Nov** (replacing "Everyone has AI now. Isn't no AI behind the times?", which made Norcoach sound against technology) **· Can I still use AI for my own work?** Of course. Use whatever helps you plan, write or run the business. Norcoach just never writes to your clients in your name: what they read is from you. It does the arithmetic (bests, trends, next week's weights) and leaves the coaching to you.

**I've switched apps before. Moving everyone was a nightmare.** As a founding coach you don't move anyone. We bring your clients and programmes over with you. `[Decision: the migration offer, and what can be imported from which apps; promise client history only where it's real]`

**What if it breaks mid-workout?** Every set saves the moment it's entered, one by one. Nothing waits for the end of the session. **NEW 7 Nov:** `[Proof: an honest record, e.g. uptime since launch and sets lost (none), once we have it]`

**Will anyone answer if something's wrong?** Yes. You write to us inside Norcoach and a person who builds it answers. **NEW 7 Nov:** Not a canned reply about clearing your cache. `[Decision: the response-time promise]`

**NEW 7 Nov · Another app already tells me who needs me.** Some write you a summary of it. Norcoach gives you the thing itself, the video, the note, the check-in, open and ready to answer, and what your client gets back carries your face and your words.

**Do my clients need a separate food app?** No, they don't need one. Norcoach has an extensive food library built in for your clients to use.

**What does it cost?** Founding coaches start with a 14-day free trial, then a founding price that never goes up for them. No paid add-ons. We'll share the price on your call. `[Decision: the price; pricing, no add-ons]`

**Is my clients' data safe?** Your clients' data is yours: only you and your clients see it, and you can export all of it whenever you like. Payments go through Stripe and card details are never stored by us.
