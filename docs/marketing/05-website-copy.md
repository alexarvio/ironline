## 2. The stakes `[Belief 1]`

### Clients don't leave because the programme was wrong. They leave because they felt unseen.

Read what clients write about online coaching and it's the same line again and again: *"Feels like they say the same thing every week."* *"No one there even knows who I am."*

You know that's not you. But past a certain number of clients, it's how it can feel from their side.

And it's expensive. Most online clients stay a few months. Keep one client a year longer at €180 a month and that's over €2,000 you didn't have to find again.

---

## 3. Recognition `[Belief 2]`

### It worked at ten clients. Then it didn't.

At five clients you saw everything. Every set, every check-in, every message. You noticed the knee, the stall, the bad week before they told you.

Then you got good at this, and more people wanted you.

Now Sunday evening is check-ins. You open a sheet, scroll to this week, read the weights and the RPE, row by row, then the next client's sheet, then the next. You scroll WhatsApp for the form video from Tuesday. Someone asks which programme they're on.

You're not a worse coach than you were at five clients. You're just one person, reading spreadsheets.

---

## 4. The real reason `[Belief 3]`

### The signs are all there. They're just scattered.

Take one ordinary week:

- **Tom** has done 80 kg for 8 on squat three weeks running. *It's in row 41 of his sheet.*
- **Sara** said her knee hurt again. *It's in WhatsApp, under 30 other messages.*
- **James** sent his deadlift on Tuesday. *Somewhere in a chat.*
- **Emma** didn't send her check-in. *The form doesn't know who didn't send.*

Every one of those is a client who needs you this week. You'll find them, if you go looking, client by client. At forty clients, there's no time to look.

That's the problem. Not your effort. **The signal is scattered, and nothing brings it to you.**

---

## 5. Why switching didn't fix it `[Belief 4]`

### Most coaching apps moved your sheets behind one login. You still have to go looking.

Maybe you tried one. Programmes in one place, a client app, a chat. Better than the sheet, at first.

But you still open each client, one by one, check each log, and build the picture yourself. More screens, the same Sunday. And when it crashes mid-workout and wipes a client's sets, you're the one who answers for it.

You don't need more screens. You need the signs to come to you.

## 6. How NorCoach works `[Belief 5]`

### Every signal comes to you, already worked out.

**Every set, counted.**
Your clients log each set on their phone, in a few taps. NorCoach works out their bests, their strength trend on every lift and every muscle group, and next week's weights from what they actually lifted. You read one line, not forty rows.
`[REC 2]` a client logs a set; the coach's view lights up a PB and a rising trend.

**Needs you.**
One list of what you owe, in order: a reply, a video to review, a check-in note, an empty week. It clears itself as you go. When it's empty, you're done. Close the laptop.
`[REC 1]`

**Links that land.**
Every message, notification and row opens the exact thing: the set, the meal, the check-in day, the photo. Not a screen to search. Your client can point to the squat they're asking about, and so can you.
`[REC 3]` a chat link opens Training with that exercise unfolded and softly lit.

**Draw & Talk.**
They send a lift. You slow it down, draw on it and talk them through it. The recording goes straight back to them, from the laptop or your phone. Like having them in front of you.
`[REC 4]` the review recorder: pen, arrow, voice, send.

**To focus on.**
After each call, up to three things go to the top of their home screen until the next one. Every day they open the app, they see what you asked of them.
`[REC 5]` the client's Home with To focus on and the next call.

**And the rest you'd expect, in the same place:** phases on one timeline, check-ins with graphs, a food diary with a clean food list, progress photos with side-by-side compare, calls, chat, invoices, and an app on your phone for chats, videos and your calendar.

## 9. FAQ (the objections, answered)

**My clients won't download another app.**
It replaces the sheet, the WhatsApp thread, the Drive folder and the food tracker for them. One app, and logging a set takes a few taps. `[Proof: Finlay's clients' adoption]`

**Spreadsheets are free.**
They are, until a client leaves because they felt unseen. One client kept a year longer pays for NorCoach many times over. And a spreadsheet can't tell you who needs you.

**I've switched apps before. Moving everyone was a nightmare.**
That's why we move your clients in with you, as a founding coach. `[Decision]`

**What if it breaks mid-workout?**
Every set saves the moment it's entered, one by one. Nothing waits for the end of the session. `[Proof: no data lost to date]`

**Will anyone answer if something's wrong?**
Yes. You write to us inside NorCoach and a person who builds it answers.

**Will it make my coaching feel automated?**
The opposite. NorCoach doesn't write to your clients for you. Every reply, every review, every drawing is yours. It takes the reading of spreadsheets off your plate, not you.

**Do my clients need MyFitnessPal?**
No. NorCoach has its own food diary, with everyday foods by the gram, at no extra cost.

**What does it cost?**
`[Decision]` For founding coaches: free while we set you up, then a founding price that never goes up. No paid add-ons for the core of your coaching.

**Is my clients' data safe?**
`[Verify before writing]` Payments go through Stripe and are never stored by us. Your clients' data is yours, and only you and your clients see it.

**Does it work where I am?**
`[Verify]` NorCoach is in English, invoices in your currency, and payments through Stripe.

# Recording scripts (for `[REC n]`)

Silent, 6 to 12 seconds, looping, made from the real app with demo clients, no real client data.
1. **Needs you:** the coach's Feed; three rows (a reply, a video, a check-in); each cleared with the round check; the list empties.
2. **Every set counted:** the client phone logs a heavier set; cut to the coach's Training row with the PB ring and the 7-day trend going up.
3. **Links that land:** a client message with a link; the coach clicks; Training opens on that exercise, unfolded, the soft highlight fading.
4. **Draw & Talk:** a squat video; the coach draws a line on the hip and an arrow, the recording timer running; Send.
5. **To focus on:** the client's phone Home: To focus on with three goals and "Your call with Finlay, Thu 10:00".
