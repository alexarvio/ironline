# Norcoach client app · design rules

Read this before touching any client screen. One hero language, one everyday language, nothing else. (The coach admin has its own: `docs/DESIGN-RULES-ADMIN.md`.)

## 1. Two languages, no third
- **Hero**: the phase cards on Home only. Full-bleed photo, dark glass panel, white type. Nothing else may look like this, with one exception: the food diary's meal cards (9 Nov, Alex; docs/boards/meal-cards-mockup.html, option A; §9). A card is the phase on now and never names the one coming next, nor does a "No plan yet" card (3 Nov, Alex: "that is something we show on events").
- **Hairline**: everything else. Thin 1px lines, rings and icons alone, light type, the page colour behind. No white cards with borders and drop shadows, no gradient fills, no tiles around icons.

**Where hairline belongs (16 Oct, Alex):** screens you read and scroll: lists, feeds, timelines (Home, Events, Meetings, Notifications, the session overview under its photo header, and the workout screen: sets on one spine, the open set as big light numerals with a thin underline instead of a box; the Training tab, shape updates, settings, the check-in screen, the food diary, Nutrition, invoices and, lightly, chat too). Pop-ups and sheets where you enter things (the check-in, measurements, Add an event, Request a meeting, the gym picker) keep their fields and boxes; don't flatten them. Since 17 Oct they speak the shared voice: a plain white sheet (no wash or glow), titles at 600, small grey uppercase labels, fields and tiles white with a hairline (ink while typing), the chosen choice ink, the thing's colour only on its icon, ring or edge, one ink pill to save.

**The one exception (16 Oct, by choice):** the Training tab's week card and its session cards keep their card shape (the done tint does real work on a to-do list). They speak the shared tokens: 500/600 type, the palette indigo and green, tints at 8–12%, a white hairline chip for Up next. Do not restyle them to hairline rows; that was tried and reverted.

## 2. Page and lines
- Page `#F5F5F5` on Home, `#F5F6F8` on the pushed screens (Events, Workout, Notifications). One per screen, never two tones on one screen.
- Home's top is paper (24 Oct, Alex: mockup A, docs/boards/home-background-mockup.html): no wash and no rings, the page's soft off-white from the top down, and the logo's star huge and very faint (4.5% ink) cropped off the top right, behind everything. It never scrolls (29 Oct, Alex): drawn on the screen's scroll box, never on what scrolls, so it stays where it is while the page moves, on Home, every chat and the coach's admin alike. When the coach has uploaded a logo, it is their logo there instead, as a one-colour shape just as faint, on Home and in the chat alike (28 Oct, Alex; docs/DESIGN-RULES-ADMIN.md §2, `--bg-mark`).
- Hairline `#E1E5EB`. Separate rows and sections with **space alone** (17 Oct, Alex): no lines between list rows, no lines over sections or above buttons (3 Nov, Alex: option A of docs/boards/row-lines-mockup.html took out the last ones: the food diary's meals, Settings, the burger menu, Invoices, the check-in's last seven days; a top bar that sticks keeps the page's tone and no line). The only line on a screen is a timeline's spine (Events, the Home events row), and a note's own underline where nothing sits right under it.
- No line under a header, a title or anything that stays on screen as the page scrolls (28 Oct, Alex: the workout's exercise name had a line between it and the coach's note; Home had the same): it is always there, so nothing needs parting from it. Before drawing a line, ask what it parts that space can't; if the answer is a heading or a pinned header, leave it out.
- White `#fff` only for a pill or a button, and it gets `inset 0 0 0 1px #E1E5EB`, never a shadow.

## 3. Type (Archivo)
- Weights **500** and **600** only. Date numerals may be **300**. Never 700 or above on a client screen.
- Every photo banner (3 Nov, Alex: the audit): the eyebrow at 600, the title at 600 and one size, 26px (a long one shrinks to fit). On one, a strip of facts (the session overview's Date | Gym | Time) is see-through with a soft white edge, its figures white at 600; a count pill (the check-in's 3 / 10) is the banner's glass pill at 600, its dot white until all are in, then green. Nutrition's ring and macro labels are the app's small label, 600, grey.
- Nutrition and the food diary are one banner (4 Nov, Alex: "if I go from this to the food diary, the background picture changes size, and the header… jumps"): exactly the top bar and 231px, never taller, the same photo and shade; the same eyebrow and title (`.nd-phase`, `.nd-kicker`, `.nd-phase-name`, 8px under the bar, the title on its 36px line); Training day | Rest day at its foot on both; a day opens on Training day unless the client picked Rest day on it and logged something there (10 Nov, Alex). The diary's date line runs on under its title, its week strip in the middle of the room left.
- Eyebrow / label: 10–11px, 600, letter-spacing .16–.18em, uppercase, `#5b6474` or the item's colour.
- Title: 15–17px, 500; the focused or live one 17–18px, 600. One line with an ellipsis, and a chevron when it is cut.
- A pushed screen's own title in its top bar (24 Oct, Alex: the bold ones read too fat, and Shape update's was another size): capitals, Archivo 15px at 600, letter-spacing .16em, ink (white over a photo), centred. One look on every screen the burger and the bell open, Shape update, the check-in and the chat's Media and files (`.cn-title`, `.ci-title`, `.cmg-head`; 24 Oct, Alex: Media and files was still the old design, now the page colour, a plain back arrow, the white hairline capsule for its tabs and files as flat rows with a line icon).
- Secondary: 12px, 500, `#8b93a1`. Tabular numerals for dates, counts and times.
- Dates and times one way (3 Nov, Alex: the audit; docs/boards/dates-times-mockup.html): the day before the month, no commas, `2 Nov`, `Mon 2 Nov`, `Monday 2 November` (British English, `en-GB`, with September short as `Sep`, never `Sept`; never `en-US`'s `Nov 2`). Times 24-hour whatever the phone is set to, `18:00`, `18:00 – 18:30`. A day's numeral in a list's date column is two figures at 22px, light (`05`), on Events and Invoices alike.
- Ink `#141a24`, body `#3a4250`, faint `#8b93a1`, label grey `#98A2B3`.

## 4. Colour
- One colour per category, everywhere (see `app/lib/eventTypes.ts`, `notificationTypes.ts`, `phases/timeline.ts`):
  training indigo `#3a3390` · nutrition teal `#1d7a70` · lifestyle beige `#a08a5c` (tint `#efede6`; 21 Oct: one beige everywhere, not amber or grey) · trip `#2f5d8f` · supplement `#1f7a4d` · health `#b8471f` · work `#5b6472` · shape update rust `#b8471f` · meeting blue `#2f6fd6` · weight goal lifestyle beige `#a08a5c` with the target mark (2 Nov, Alex: "I like this color for the weight and also the icon"; `GOAL_COLOR`, `TARGET_ICON` in app/lib/weightGoal.ts).
- One rust and one green (3 Nov, Alex: the audit; docs/boards/colours-mockup.html): rust `#b8471f` for what needs doing, what is behind and every client error (sign-in's too); green `#2f7a3f` for what is done, the check-in's pop-up too. Numbers down a list are ink, never navy (the session overview's as Training's).
- A colour appears as a thin ring, an icon, a label or a 2px line. Never as a filled block behind text.
- Every line chart one way (4 Nov, Alex; docs/DESIGN-RULES-ADMIN.md §8): one smooth curve through every point (`monotoneCurve`), no dot on each point, the latest a dot pulsing, hovering a faint ink guide, an ink ring and a white hairline pill kept off the line.
- Tints: `rgba(rgb, .12)` at most. Past things go grey (`grayscale(1)`) and fade to 30–45%; future things keep their colour at ~55%; the live or focused one is at 100%.

## 5. Icons
- The existing category icons, drawn at **stroke 1.6**, round caps and joins, no fills. Icons from different sets in one list draw at one weight (3 Nov, Alex: the burger's, 1.6 on the 24 grid, 1.2 on the 18 grid).
- An icon centred in a ring or a round button sits on whole pixels (28 Oct, Alex): the icon's size and the ring's both even or both odd, so the room either side is whole (a 22px ring, a 12px icon), and the drawing centred in its viewBox.
- The icon alone, in a thin ring: no tile, no disc, no halo. On the Events screen and Home's events timeline every event wears its ring (27 Oct, Alex): one still to come in its category's colour at the row's softer strength, one that is over in grey (`#C9CFD8`), the focused one in full; only what is on today pulses, in its colour.
- A note is marked by a pen writing on a line (4 Nov, Alex: option A of docs/boards/note-icon-mockup.html): one `NoteIcon` (app/components/icons.tsx, `NOTE_ICON_PATHS` where a path is wanted) everywhere, client, coach and phone: Your note, the workout's ⋯ note row, Note to {client}, the Call notes badge. A note is the pen whoever wrote it, the client's own on the coach's screens too (4 Nov, Alex: "pen too"). An Edit stays the pencil; a message in the chat stays the chat icon.

## 6. Buttons and controls
- Primary: ink pill `#141a24`, white text, height 38–44, 600. One per screen at most.
- Secondary: white pill with the hairline inset, ink text.
- Text action: 12–13px, 600, ink or `#5b6474`, no underline.
- Facts in a row are parted by a short drawn upright bar, never a middle dot (21 Oct, Alex: the workout's targets, `15 min | 6 km/h | 8%`), as the coach admin does: `Bars` (app/client/Bars.tsx, `.cl-sep`), the one bar on every client screen, never a local copy (3 Nov, Alex: Notifications, the check-in Calendar and the sign-in foot drew their own), e.g. a session's `Wed 21 Oct | 46 min | Muscle Factory` and an extra set's `Set 3 | Extra` (23 Oct, Alex).
- The workout's targets are a table (3 Nov, Alex: A of docs/boards/workout-targets-table-mockup.html, "dynamic… if the coach sets the sets, reps and kg we only show 3"): `Target`, then one column for each figure the coach filled in for that exercise and only those (Sets, Reps, kg or lbs, RPE, Tempo, Rest; a cardio's Time and Distance), the names in small grey caps over one hairline across the width, the figures under them in ink at 18px/600, left in their columns; `Target` stays on the left and the columns share the width evenly, however many there are (9 Nov, Alex; `.lw-target-grid`). Tempo and Rest reach the workout screen this way.
- That table is the one drawing of a target everywhere (3 Nov, Alex: docs/boards/drawn-several-ways-mockup.html): the exercise page, the session overview's opened exercise and cardio and the cardio page, one order (Sets, Reps with an en dash, kg or lbs in the exercise's unit, RPE, Tempo, Rest; a cardio's Time, Pace, Incline, Distance), from one helper (`targetColumns`, `cardioColumns`, `TargetTable` in workoutShared.tsx); six or more columns tighten to fit a phone (16px figures, 8px apart).
- A swapped exercise or cardio is a naked sheet (4 Nov, Alex: "when we swap an exercise, it should just be a naked sheet"): no target table and no planned figures, on the exercise page, the cardio page and the session overview alike; its name and `Instead of {name}` stay, a set on it is never asked as far off (6 Nov, Alex: another machine "is going to feel heavier or less heavy"), and its sets start empty (the latest set done this session, never the coach's figures for the exercise it replaced); the coach's note on what it replaced goes there too (their note on the substitute shows), and no change against last time, which was another lift. The coach's side is unchanged.
- One done check (3 Nov, Alex): `DoneCheck` (app/components/DoneCheck.tsx), green `#2f7a3f` at full strength, a 1.5px ring and the check at 1.6, ring and check both even (22→12, 32→14, 36→16): a done session on Training (32), a done exercise or cardio in the session overview (22, in its number column), a done set (36; a warm-up the same in amber, an extra set its dashed edge in green), the food diary's day (22). Never a typed ✓.
- Leaving with something not sent asks in place, never the browser's own alert (3 Nov, Alex): Shape update's photos picked and not sent turn its send row into `Leave without sending 2 photos?` with the white `Keep` and the rust `Leave`, as the check-in's `Discard changes?`.
- Adding a photo or video, anywhere, opens the phone's own picker in one tap (4 Nov, Alex: "make it a rule"): on an iPhone its Photo Library | Take Photo or Video | Choose File, never a menu of ours before it (Take a photo | Choose from library was two menus for one photo), never a see-through file field laid over an icon (it takes two taps on an iPhone): a plain button that calls a hidden file field's `.click()`, no `capture`, so the camera stays one of the phone's choices. The chat's + (a menu only where there is more than a file to add, an event), the food diary's camera, Shape update's angles (each angle's word on how to take it under How to take them). A hover look on such a button or tile only under `@media (hover: hover)`, so a tap never leaves it changed.
- A session going on, back on Training (3 Nov, Alex: "the whole design is with the hairline"): the white hairline pill over the tab bar, no shadow, the live dot green, `Lower` in ink over `In progress | Muscle Factory` grey (3 Nov, Alex: the name alone on top, never cut), the clock in ink, Resume the ink pill. Ended, the saved message comes in the same place in the same pill for four seconds: the done check drawn in its green ring, `Lower saved`, then `46 min | 18 sets` grey; no sentence (`.tr-resume`, `.tr-toast`).
- A set far off its exercise (27 Oct, Alex: 608 kg typed for 17.5, 42 reps for 12) waits under its numbers, unsaved, until the client answers: a weight over 40% (and 5 kg) above the best last time (else the target), reps over double the target's top. The words in rust (`608 kg is far over last time, 17.5 kg.`), `Yes, 608 kg` the small ink pill, `I meant lbs` the white hairline pill (reads the figure in lbs and puts the exercise in lbs). Lighter or fewer is never asked; asked at most once an exercise a session (27 Oct, Alex: "we should not have to answer yes every time"): a Yes settles the weight (or the reps) for the rest of it, and a set already logged today raises the bar for the others. While the question waits the set is not done: no Saved, not counted, nothing moves on. So nothing (the weight rule, next week) builds on a slip.
- The rest timer counts down (3 Nov, Alex: "everytime I press it I get the option of 1 min 2 min and 3 min"): a tap on the bar's `Rest timer` opens `1 min`, `2 min` and `3 min` over it as white hairline pills, the pills alone with no card behind them (4 Nov, Alex) (and `Stop` in rust while one runs, the running one ink); a pick starts it, the pill ink with `Rest 1:42` going down; at zero one buzz and `Rest done` in green for a moment, then `Rest timer` again (`RestTimer.tsx`).
- The open set's two big numerals sit as a pair centred on the row, each on the right of its line and typed from the right (9 Nov, Alex: "only centering… the current set… if its collapsed move it to the left"), its unit (`KG`, `REPS`) in the same small caps on the figure's right, on its row, never under the line (10 Nov, Alex); a collapsed set's figures stay on the left, by the spine. `Next up` names only what is ahead (9 Nov, Alex): never one skipped earlier, though Next still goes back to it; the last exercise says `Last exercise`, and one with only earlier ones left says nothing.
- Adding to a week (9 Nov, Alex: "do a workout twice in a week"): under the week's sessions one + on the spine, no words, opening a pop-up in the gym picker's voice: `Rogue session` or `Duplicate a session`, the second then this week's planned sessions to pick from, then the gym as a start asks it (one gym is no question), and straight into the workout, no overview before it (9 Nov, Alex). The pop-up's ✕ is on its right. A duplicate is the session's own exercises, targets and cardio as they stand (no swap, no warm-ups), a row after the week's with the copy mark after its name, trained as the session is (gym, last time, the weight rule), counted in none of the plan's figures and never missed. Left unstarted, or discarded, it goes. Everything it logs counts as any session's (9 Nov, Alex: Muscle balance, Strength, PBs, the weight rule, its gym, the coach's reviews of its photos and videos). Needs signal, as a rogue session does (`duplicateSession`, `copy_of`, `AddSessionSheet`).
- A rogue session adds up but is never compared (9 Nov, Alex: "they should be added… but since it is like a one time thing we can't really compare the data"): its sets count on Muscle balance's Sets (by a name the library knows) and the muscle map, its day as trained on Consistency and Nutrition, and its session, sets and time at its gym, which it asks as a session does; it is in no Strength, last time, Last session or 30 days, PB or weight rule.
- Where you are in a workout (27 Oct, Alex, from the gym): the set being logged has its ink ring pulsing in ink, and the exercise on screen fills its segment of the top bar in training purple, pulsing, so the bar shows where you are as well as what is done. The pulse is never cut off (28 Oct, Alex): a fold clips only while its height eases.
- Warm-ups (28 Oct, Alex): last time's warm-up comes back only from the same lift (not a session where it was swapped, not another variation). Once every set of the exercise is in, a warm-up logged stays, done in amber; one left untouched goes, and stays gone when the workout is looked back on.
- Training somewhere else once (6 Nov, Alex: a day pass on holiday, "just going to be this one time"): the gym picker's `One-time gym` (9 Nov, Alex: was Just this once) beside `Add a gym`, the same form with the name and its picture alone (no colour or type), `Start here` (`Switch here` mid-session) going straight to the session. That gym is never in the picker's list again but on its own session, never where a session starts, and never planned for by the coach; its sets keep their own weights as every gym's (`once`, `GymPickerDialog`; the session keeps its pick, `gym_id`, before a set is logged). The picker's rows end on `One-time gym` and `Add a gym`, side by side sharing the width half and half (9 Nov, Alex). The picker only picks or adds (9 Nov, Alex: a long press there deleted a main gym as the finger lifted); a gym is changed from Settings.
- Every toggle slides (28 Oct, Alex: "whenever we have a toggle across norcoach the toggle works like this one", the workout's KG | LBS): a capsule's picked pill eases to the option picked over 250ms, never a jump; a switch's knob eases the same. One mechanism for the whole app, `SlidePills` (app/components/SlidePills.tsx, "Sliding pills" at the end of globals.css): a new capsule joins by its row's class in `SLIDE_ROWS` or `data-slide` on its row, its options the row's direct children, the picked one marked (`aria-checked`, `aria-pressed`, `aria-selected`, or `on`). Details' and New client's Gender is one too (29 Oct, Alex). Only capsules slide (28 Oct, Alex: "only capsule slides"): a row of separate white chips (the Events filter, the metric chips, Compare's angles) and tile pickers swap at once.
- Every client capsule is one size (3 Nov, Alex: the audit; docs/boards/toggles-audit-mockup.html): 36px tall, 3px of air round the pill, 13px words at 600 picked or not, so nothing shifts. Training day | Rest day on Nutrition and the food diary keeps its white underline tabs over the photo (3 Nov, Alex: a white capsule there "does not blend in"), the underline gliding to the day picked as every toggle slides, with no page dots under the rings. The food diary's week strip marks days as every calendar does: today's numeral on a disc, the picked day ringed and no dot under it (its ring says it), the days to come faint (white over its photo), never a tile; a day with something logged keeps its small dot.
- `− Remove a set` beside `+ Add a set` (10 Nov, Alex: "when they add a set we have now a +1 so in this case it would be a -1"): it takes the last set on the list, so no set changes its number, never the only one; a set with figures in it asks in the same room first (`Remove set 4?`, Cancel and Remove in words), an empty one goes at once. It is for this session alone: the Target table keeps the coach's count, next week is as they set it, and the exercise is done at the sets left. A set taken off comes back first on `+ Add a set`, before anything counts as extra. `setsDue` (app/lib/setsDue.ts), `sets_removed`; on a rogue session it just changes the count. The coach sees `−1` (docs/DESIGN-RULES-ADMIN.md §8).
- Logging a set (25 Oct, Alex: "the weight does not save, and the next one is always the default"; 29 Oct, Alex: "it should always show the weight and reps done last time"): each set opens on the weight and reps that set was done at last time (the same lift: not a swap, not another variation), met or missed, at the gym you are at whenever the lift has been done there, another gym's only when it never has (9 Nov, Alex: 110 from one gym opened under another's 100); the coach's weight is in the targets over the sets. A set with no last time opens on the latest set done this session, else the coach's figures. Reps put in while the weight still shows the plan in grey take that weight, and the set saves. A weight typed and the reps stepped straight after both count. Each set says how it compares with the same set last time (2 Nov, Alex: "if I add 80 kg and I had 75… +5 kg… 10 reps instead of 12, that is −2 reps"): small at its top right, 600, green up and rust down (an assisted lift's weight the other way), only what changed; a done set `+5 kg  −2 reps` after its figures, one after the other on one line (2 Nov, Alex), the open one `+5` and `−2` at each figure's top right once typed. No last time (or a swap), nothing. The weight rule moves the coach's weight up for the weeks after only on a set heavier than it whose reps sit inside its range (4 Nov, Alex: "it has to have a higher weight, but it also needs to be within the range"; 10 Nov, Alex: "keep it within rep range"; 70 kg × 8 or × 15 against 10–12 moves nothing, 60 × 10 moves it to 60; a single figure is a goal to reach, 12 or more against `12`; no percentage and no fixed step, the weight lifted is the new weight; `repWindow`, app/lib/targetRule.ts), and the coach's own figure is kept beside a raised one (`coach_kg`).
- Nothing logged in the gym is lost (7 Nov, Alex: "Build offline saving"; the words below are a proposal until Alex settles them): every save while training (a set, a warm-up, a note, a swap, the cardio, the gym, the start, the end, a discard, Couldn't train) is kept on the phone until the server has it, goes by itself when the signal or the app comes back, in the order made and once, and the screen keeps showing it meanwhile, never the older figures (`workoutOutbox.ts`, `saveWorkoutAction`). The open set says `Saving`, then `Not sent yet` in grey once it has waited 3s or the phone is offline, `Saved` once sent; a session row with something still to send has `Not sent yet` grey after its facts; what the server refused is the row's one rust line, what and why (`2 sets not saved | Finish Upper first`), gone once the row is opened, and a set it refused says `Not saved` in rust on the open set, not done, a tap sending it again. The end's pill says `Lower saved` with the done check only once the server has it; while it waits, `Lower | 46 min | 18 sets | Not sent yet` with no check (`Changes | Not sent yet` after Save changes). Nothing hangs on the signal: the swipe to end, Save changes and Couldn't train carry on at once. A rogue session, a new gym and photos or videos still need signal.
- A cardio page has one button, Mark as done (9 Nov, Alex): `Substitute today` is in its ⋯, as an exercise's is.
