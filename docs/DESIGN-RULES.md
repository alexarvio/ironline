# NorCoach client app · design rules

Read this before touching any client screen. One hero language, one everyday language, nothing else. (The coach admin has its own: `docs/DESIGN-RULES-ADMIN.md`.)

## 1. Two languages, no third
- **Hero**: the phase cards on Home only. Full-bleed photo, dark glass panel, white type. Nothing else may look like this.
- **Hairline**: everything else. Thin 1px lines, rings and icons alone, light type, the page colour behind. No white cards with borders and drop shadows, no gradient fills, no tiles around icons.

**Where hairline belongs (16 Oct, Alex):** screens you read and scroll: lists, feeds, timelines (Home, Events, Meetings, Notifications, the session overview under its photo header, and the workout screen: sets on one spine, the open set as big light numerals with a thin underline instead of a box; the Training tab, shape updates, settings, the check-in screen, the food diary, Nutrition, invoices and, lightly, chat too). Pop-ups and sheets where you enter things (the check-in, measurements, Add an event, Request a meeting, the gym picker) keep their fields and boxes; don't flatten them. Since 17 Oct they speak the shared voice: a plain white sheet (no wash or glow), titles at 600, small grey uppercase labels, fields and tiles white with a hairline (ink while typing), the chosen choice ink, the thing's colour only on its icon, ring or edge, one ink pill to save.

**The one exception (16 Oct, by choice):** the Training tab's week card and its session cards keep their card shape (the done tint does real work on a to-do list). They speak the shared tokens: 500/600 type, the palette indigo and green, tints at 8–12%, a white hairline chip for Up next. Do not restyle them to hairline rows; that was tried and reverted.

## 2. Page and lines
- Page `#F5F5F5` on Home, `#F5F6F8` on the pushed screens (Events, Workout, Notifications). One per screen, never two tones on one screen.
- Home's top is paper (24 Oct, Alex: mockup A, docs/boards/home-background-mockup.html): no wash and no rings, the page's soft off-white from the top down, and the logo's star huge and very faint (4.5% ink) cropped off the top right, behind everything.
- Hairline `#E1E5EB`. Separate rows and sections with **space alone** (17 Oct, Alex): no lines between list rows, no lines over sections or above buttons. The only line on a screen is a timeline's spine (Events, the Home events row), and a note's own underline where nothing sits right under it.
- White `#fff` only for a pill or a button, and it gets `inset 0 0 0 1px #E1E5EB`, never a shadow.

## 3. Type (Archivo)
- Weights **500** and **600** only. Date numerals may be **300**. Never 700 or above on a client screen.
- Eyebrow / label: 10–11px, 600, letter-spacing .16–.18em, uppercase, `#5b6474` or the item's colour.
- Title: 15–17px, 500; the focused or live one 17–18px, 600. One line with an ellipsis, and a chevron when it is cut.
- A pushed screen's own title in its top bar (24 Oct, Alex: the bold ones read too fat, and Shape update's was another size): capitals, Archivo 15px at 600, letter-spacing .16em, ink (white over a photo), centred. One look on every screen the burger and the bell open, Shape update, the check-in and the chat's Media and files (`.cn-title`, `.ci-title`, `.cmg-head`; 24 Oct, Alex: Media and files was still the old design, now the page colour, a plain back arrow, the white hairline capsule for its tabs and files as flat rows with a line icon).
- Secondary: 12px, 500, `#8b93a1`. Tabular numerals for dates, counts and times.
- Ink `#141a24`, body `#3a4250`, faint `#8b93a1`, label grey `#98A2B3`.

## 4. Colour
- One colour per category, everywhere (see `app/lib/eventTypes.ts`, `notificationTypes.ts`, `phases/timeline.ts`):
  training indigo `#3a3390` · nutrition teal `#1d7a70` · lifestyle beige `#a08a5c` (tint `#efede6`; 21 Oct: one beige everywhere, not amber or grey) · trip `#2f5d8f` · supplement `#1f7a4d` · health `#b8471f` · work `#5b6472` · shape update rust `#b8471f` · meeting blue `#2f6fd6`.
- A colour appears as a thin ring, an icon, a label or a 2px line. Never as a filled block behind text.
- Tints: `rgba(rgb, .12)` at most. Past things go grey (`grayscale(1)`) and fade to 30–45%; future things keep their colour at ~55%; the live or focused one is at 100%.

## 5. Icons
- The existing category icons, drawn at **stroke 1.6**, round caps and joins, no fills.
- The icon alone. A ring around it only when the item is live or in focus; a live ring pulses in its colour. No tile, no disc, no halo.

## 6. Buttons and controls
- Primary: ink pill `#141a24`, white text, height 38–44, 600. One per screen at most.
- Secondary: white pill with the hairline inset, ink text.
- Text action: 12–13px, 600, ink or `#5b6474`, no underline.
- Facts in a row are parted by a short drawn upright bar, never a middle dot (21 Oct, Alex: the workout's targets, `15 min | 6 km/h | 8%`), as the coach admin does: `Bars` (app/client/Bars.tsx, `.cl-sep`), e.g. a session's `Wed 21 Oct | 46 min | Muscle Factory` and an extra set's `Set 3 | Extra` (23 Oct, Alex).
- Logging a set (25 Oct, Alex: "the weight does not save, and the next one is always the default"): the first set opens on the coach's weight; every set after it on the weight of the latest set done this session. Reps put in while the weight still shows the plan in grey take that weight, and the set saves. A weight typed and the reps stepped straight after both count.
- The workout's finish list (21 Oct, Alex): a weighted exercise with sets logged shows the unit it was logged in before its Done, `kg` or `lbs` alone (23 Oct, Alex: not both), the word alone in ink as Done is (no pill); a tap switches it, the exercise's logged sets with it, and it opens in that unit next time. A cardio marked done just says `Done` in green (a tap still takes it back), no "tap to undo". Marking it done moves on to what is next after 1.4s, as an exercise does once its last set is in (23 Oct, Alex); taking it back in that time stays.
- A pill that opens a list (Saved days, Supplements; 17 Oct): closed, the white hairline pill; open, the pill stays as the header and the list drops out under it flat on the page, tied to the pill's icon by a thin line down the left that runs behind the rows. No white box round the list.
- Segments and chips: white with the hairline, the active one ink with white text.
- Anything on/off (17 Oct, Alex): white while off, ink while on. The rest timer, KG/LBS, filter chips, Settings switches, the current tab in the nav. An action like Demo is not a toggle: it stays a white secondary pill.
- Inside a row, an action is its words alone (Fill in, Edit, Use, View), no pill.
- A note the client writes (17 Oct, Alex), in a pop-up or on a page: no box, the words on a hairline underline (`#C9CFD8`, ink while typing), 16px at 500. Cancel is a text action, Save or Send a small ink pill. Short fields like numbers, names and search keep their white hairline box. Exception: in a pop-up where Save sits right under the note (check-in, add event), no underline at all; it read as a bar between them.
- Exceptions by choice: the indigo Live chip and the navy-tinted Up next chip on Training.
- No navy fills, no gradients, no shadows under buttons.

## 7. Motion
- 200–250ms ease for size and colour. Scroll-driven focus follows the finger live.
- Snap to one spot; the item in the spot grows (ring ×1.25–1.3, title to 17–18px) and nothing else moves. Rows keep a fixed height so content below never jumps.
- Only live things pulse. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## 8. Layout
- Section eyebrow at 28px in from the screen edge, content 16px in, rows may bleed edge to edge.
- Sections vs. items (16 Oct): a section label (Events, Latest from, Meetings) is ink `#141a24` at 11.5px and sits under a hairline 16px in from the screen edge, with room above. Labels inside a section stay grey, and lines inside a section are inset (40px), so they read as a level below.
- Dates in a left column as light numerals, one date per day, the live one dated today.
- To focus on (23 Oct, Alex): the coach's goals until the next call, the first thing on Home, right under the greeting and above Today and the phase cards; whenever there are goals (24 Oct, Alex: they stay until changed, or replaced when the coach completes the next call with new ones). Its ink label straight under the greeting, no hairline over it (24 Oct, Alex), `from {Coach}` with their face (20px) on the right. Each goal a bullet point (23 Oct, Alex: "not a to-do list… just bullet points"), nothing to tick or tap, drawn as a spine to the call (24 Oct, Alex: mockup B, docs/boards/to-focus-on-v2.html): each goal a small ink ring on one grey line, its words at 16px/500, and the line ends at the call, a ring in the call's blue with the camera, pulsing in that blue while the call is booked, as a live event's ring (25 Oct, Alex; the rings drawn as SVG on whole pixels, so no side reads fatter), `Your call with {Coach}` at 600 and `Thu 29 Oct | 10:00` grey under it (`Today`, `Tomorrow` in the day's place; 24 Oct, Alex: no "in 5 days"). No call booked: the goals still show, the line running between them alone. Classes `hm-focus` (app/client/home/FocusList.tsx).
- Phase goals are off (23 Oct, Alex: To focus on replaces them; `FEATURES.phaseGoals` in app/lib/features.ts): no Objectives from {Coach} on the phase cards, Training, Nutrition or the check-in, and a phase's starts row on Events has nothing more, so it doesn't open. The stored goals are kept.
- No position dots (except under the Home phase cards, brought back 16 Oct by Alex), no "See all" when a tap on the item already leads there, no explanation sentences.

Units are metric: `g` first and where a new food opens, `ml` and `l` for a liquid, then its portions as `1 slice (30 g)`; no oz, lb or cups where a portion says it.
- The burger (23 Oct, Alex): Meetings, Events, Shape update (once the coach asks for an angle or there are sheets), Invoices, Settings, Help.
- Settings · Card pictures (23 Oct, Alex): the tracks by name, each with the chevron after it; a tap folds it open in place on its pictures (one open at a time): the client's own in order, a round × asking in place (Remove over the dimmed photo), a white hairline Add tile up to six, `Use {Coach}'s picture` in words; each change saved at once. Side by side: two 3:4 photos, no Before/After, a missing one `Not taken` on a dashed hairline, a tap opens it big. Slider: one 3:4 stage, the left sheet over the right cut at a white line with a round white handle, dragged sideways anywhere (an up-and-down swipe still scrolls) or stepped with the arrows, the two sheets as small pills in its lower corners. - A link lands on its thing (24 Oct, Alex: "the id on every message… good but not sharp yet"). - Every chat works one way (25 Oct, Alex: "the chats need to have a rule set and they all follow it"): the client's chat, the coach's phone and the laptop's chat panel. A thread opens on its newest message and stays there while pictures load: only the coach scrolling moves it off the end.
- A list over time is the Events-screen itinerary (`evp-` classes): white pill on top to add or ask, one spine, light date numerals, a snap spot that grows its row, the past greyed under a PAST label. A row opens only when it has more; then it gets a chevron.
  - Events: a Today row always, even when nothing (shown) is on: an empty ink ring with no icon, "Nothing on today" (or "No trips today" under a filter).
  - Notifications: flat rows with a hairline between them (inset to the text), the ink dot and a 600 title for unread, 500 once read. ## 10. The coach's phone (`/coach`, 21 Oct, Alex: built from the mockup)
- Three things only: every client's chat, the videos clients send from an exercise, and the coach's Calendar (22 Oct, Alex). Planning and editing stay on the laptop. Classes `cph-` (app/coach/coach.css).
- This file's language: the pushed screens' page, Archivo 500/600, ink. Chats, Videos and Calendar on a floating white tab bar, the tab on screen in ink, an ink count on Chats and Videos.
- Chats: newest first, a client a row, space between rows; unread is the name and time at 600, the line in body grey, and an ink count. A photo or video sent last shows its icon then `Photo` or `Video` (with words, the icon then the words; a voice message and a file the same), as the laptop's list (`ChatLastLine`, 22 Oct, Alex).
- A chat: theirs white with a hairline on the left, the coach's a neutral tint on the right. A message's menu is the chats' one (rule below). + sends a photo, video or file, the mic a voice message, the ink arrow the words.
- Videos: Waiting (a wait past a day in rust), then Replied, greyed. Each row shows the photo or the video's first frame, the wait as `9 days ago` (no `Photo`), and while waiting two round icons on its right (25 Oct, Alex): Review (the pen) and Done (the check), Done taking it off Waiting, the Feed and the dots without a reply to the client, down to Replied as `Done`; a new file from the client opens it again (`closed_at`). A video opens on the recorder, as the laptop's review does: the tools on the dark stage, one ink Record review, a few words on the underline instead. While recording, the client's video plays at half volume, in the speaker and the take, and the coach's voice sits over it (25 Oct, Alex: "we always hear ourselves good").
- Calendar (22 Oct, Alex: the laptop's, for a thumb; `CoachCalendar`, `EntrySheet`): the same entries and the laptop's own actions, so a change shows on both. `October 2026` with ‹ Today › over a week strip (today's numeral on an ink disc, the chosen day ringed in ink, a short 2px line in each entry's colour under its day, a clash rust); a swipe on the strip is another week, on the day a day. The day as `Today 22/10` (else its weekday) with the count, then its entries as a list: the times on the left, 24-hour (`09:00` over a grey `09:30`, `All day`), the entry in its category's colour as the laptop draws it (calendar/chrome.ts), its title first and the client under it (23 Oct, Alex), a double-booking rust with `Overlaps Ben 10:15–11:00`; NOW the ink line with its pulsing dot. The double-bookings still to come sit over the week with `Open 30/10`. A tap opens the entry's sheet: what, its category in its colour, the facts, the client, the note, then Chat with {First} (a client's call), Edit and Remove (asks first). + in the head adds one on the chosen day: the laptop's fields in its order, the day, the times and the client each picked on a page of the sheet of their own (the day on `MonthRange`). With only on a Client call or Training (23 Oct, Alex): on another category the row isn't there and the client is dropped, as on the laptop; the rows under it move up, no blank room kept (25 Oct, Alex), and Link the same off a Client call. The sheet keeps the height of its fullest category, so the category tiles never move when another is picked (25 Oct, Alex): the room a shorter one leaves is under the note, Cancel and Add at its foot; no row is ever squeezed. A Client call's sheet has Link, typed as What is, and the entry's sheet a Join call row when it has one, never the URL (25 Oct, Alex).
- Notifications: a switch in the account sheet (the coach's face), white while off and ink while on. A push is the client's name over their first line (or `Video of Squat`); a tap opens that chat or video. None for a chat open on the phone.
