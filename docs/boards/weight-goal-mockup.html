Weight goals

A weight on a date, e.g. 82 kg by 20 Dec, that the coach or the client can set; several in a row make milestones. Three places it could live. They all share one rule underneath, so the choice is only where it shows.

On track, the rule (same for all three). A straight planned line runs from where they were when the goal was set (90 kg on 5/10) to the goal (82 kg on 20/12). Their last 7 days' average is compared with where that line is today. Within 0.8 kg of it: On track; further along: Ahead; short of it: Behind. Under a week in: Too early. Past the date: Reached or Missed by 1.2 kg. It is the plan you agreed, not a forecast, so nothing predicts where the weight is heading.

To settle before building

  1. Where it lives: A, B, C, or a mix. My pick: C as its home (it is date-shaped, like an event) plus B's dashed line and verdict on the weight charts, so wherever you look at weight you see the goal.
  2. The dashed planned line runs into the future. It is the agreed plan, not a prediction, but you took the prediction off Home's Weight on 23 Oct, so say if it still reads like one.
  3. Clients set their own goals freely, or the coach approves them first? (A client goal shows up for the coach as a Read item either way.)
  4. Colour and mark: lifestyle beige with a target icon (weight is a lifestyle metric).
