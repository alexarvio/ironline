A call that repeats

Mockup with sample words, Schedule a meeting. Nothing here is built yet. Today: pick a date on the month, then Weekly from a list, and the weekday is whichever date was clicked. Here: say it the way you think it, “every Tuesday at 09:00”.

A. Repeat first, then the day of the week

Repeat is the capsule at the top of the right side. Once: the date and time as today. Weekly or more: the weekdays as chips, the time, how many calls; the first call is the next Tuesday by itself, and the month lights every call. A grey line says it back in words.

B. As a sentence

The repeat is written as you would say it, each word a field to tap: how often, the day, the time, from when, how many. Picking Once turns the sentence back into a date and a time. Fewer controls, but each one is a small menu.

In both

  • The weekday and the time are what you set; the first call is the next one of that weekday, on its own (today’s Wednesday 11/11, so Tuesday 17/11). A tap on another day on the month moves the first call and its weekday with it.
  • Every call in the series lights on the month, so you see them before you save.
  • The button is the verb alone, Schedule, the count said in the grey line instead.
  • Every 2 days could join the Repeat choices too if you want it (Every day, Every 2 days); then the weekdays step away.
