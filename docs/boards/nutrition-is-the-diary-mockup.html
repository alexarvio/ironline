Tap Nutrition and you are in the diary for today, logging. No word to find, no ring to guess. The banner is the one banner both screens share already (photo, phase, date, week strip, Training day | Rest day).

The Nutrition tab opens on the food diary exactly as it is today. The ring and macros, which show what is eaten against the targets, open the overview behind it (Supplements, Water, Finlay's note, Last 7 days), the other way round from now. Nothing is redrawn; the two screens just swap places.
