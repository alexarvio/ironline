Draft rules: event and phase pop-ups

Once approved these go into the admin design rules, and every pop-up about an event or a phase follows them (the Plan, the Phases screen, the Events card).

  1. One card. The `.dlh` card, 480px wide. Title 18px/600; the plain round ✕ (28px, grey), never on a disc.
  2. One tag row under the title: the category or track tag in its colour, then the state tag: Live green, Scheduled blue, Draft peach, Past grey. Events get the state too, so an event and a phase read the same.
  3. Who added it is not a tag. It sits bottom left: their face and "Added by Finlay" ("Added by you"). Phases leave it out.
  4. The span, always in one place: the date tiles on the left (month over day, weekday under; one tile for a single day), and on the right how long (ink, 600) over where it stands (grey): Day 2 of 7, Week 3 of 13, Ended 21/10. A single day has no length (24 Oct, Alex: one tile says it): where it stands alone, in ink (In 6 days, Today, 3 days ago).
  5. The track under the span shows where today is, in the category's colour: a phase in week segments, an event as one line. None for a single day, and empty for one still to come.
  6. A note under its small caps NOTE label, only when there is one; its words 14px/500.
  7. The footer: the one action on the right. Open on a view; on a change, Cancel in words and an ink Save.
  8. Move: the same head as the view (title, tags), then the change table alone: old struck grey, NEW in the category's colour, a value that stays in ink; Date for a single day, Start, End and Length for a span. No picture of it and no line saying what changed (24 Oct, Alex).
  9. Never "something". An event with no title is named by its category: "Move family event", "Family event".
  10. Dates as the rest of the admin: tiles for the span, d/m in the table and the axis (the year only when it isn't this one).
