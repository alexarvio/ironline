Home, under Latest from. The same three goals each time, nothing to tick (they are what to keep in mind until the next call). Colour is the call's blue, as the To focus on notification's badge is.

My pick: D, with A close. D uses the app's own light numerals (the date column on Events and Invoices), so the list looks set rather than typed, and the blue ties it to the call it came from.
