Why today’s words read wrong

  • The call goes, with its notes and recap. Goals set in it stay. Things “go” and “stay” as if by themselves; nobody is doing anything, so it reads like a puzzle.
  • A, B and C say who does what, name the thing, and use the words the screen uses (summary, not recap; the next 5 calls, not later calls in this series).

A rule for the words, whichever layout

  • Title: the action and the thing, as a question. Delete the call “lol”?
  • Explanation: one or two short sentences, only what isn’t obvious: what else goes with it, and what stays. Never how the feature works.
  • Choice: what ticking it adds, starting with a verb or “The next…”, with its facts grey under it.
  • Action: the verb alone, Delete or Remove, never what it acts on: the title already says it (11 Nov, Alex).
