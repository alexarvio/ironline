Workout: what comes out of the ⋯

The ⋯ keeps Substitute today, Send a photo or video, Add a note for yourself and Discard: occasional things, where people look for them. Two come out onto the page because they're used most sessions, and a finished session gets back what the client sent and the coach's reply.

Today a warm-up set is only in the ⋯ (Add a warm-up set, second item).

Now

The ⋯ beside the exercise name, then Add a warm-up set. Nothing on the page says warm-ups exist until one is added.

2 · The gym capsule

The pill at the top already switches gyms on a tap, but it shows the gym's name in faint grey with nothing that says it can be tapped (Change gym is also in the ⋯).

Now

The ink pill with a purple glow: the gym's name at 65% white, then the clock. Its 700 and 900 weights and the glow are from before the hairline pass.
