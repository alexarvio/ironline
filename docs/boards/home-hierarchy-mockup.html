Home: what goes first, and how much room it takes

The same client on the same day in all three: a session today, the daily check-in due, three things to focus on, a weight goal on track, the coach's latest note, two events and a call on Thursday. The chips over each phone are its order top to bottom: rust what asks something of them today, purple the plan and where they stand. Tap any pill to open or close it.

1As it is, with the weight pill

Today's order, the weight goal added as a pill under To focus on. What they have to do today only shows inside the phase cards, so it isn't the first thing they see.

    2Now, the plan, coming up

    What today asks first: the session and the check-in as two rows, the one ink pill to start. Then the phase cards, with the coach's guidance and the weight goal as pills right under them. Events and the call at the bottom.

      3Progress up front

      Today first, then shorter phase cards and the weight always in view as a row of its own, so where they stand is seen without a tap. The coach's goals and note after it, then what's coming.
