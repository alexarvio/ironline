Needs-you rows: one anatomy

The rows from your four screenshots: the Feed's Needs you and Activity, and the Phases screen's Ending soon. Every row has the same base: date, face and name, the category's icon, the thing. After that it depends on the row: the client's own words in quotes, a bar for a phase running out, how long it has waited, and the action. The rust to-do strip on a client's tab keeps its own look.

AOne line, the log's columns

Activity's row, made the rule everywhere: the date first, then who, then the category icon in its colour before the thing, their words after it in grey. Every column lines up down the page.

BCategory ring, two lines

The category leads as a ring in its colour (as the Events screen's rows). Line one: who and the thing. Line two: the date and their words, so a long comment gets the whole width.

CGrouped by category

A's one-line row, the list split by category under small-caps heads with the icon and count: Messages, Training, Lifestyle. The row doesn't repeat the icon; the head says it.

In all three: the wait is rust past a day and grey under it; the action is words and an icon in ink, no pill; the round check to clear it shows on hover. Ending soon puts its end date in the date column, its bar where a comment would go and its days left where the wait sits.
