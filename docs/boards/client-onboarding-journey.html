A new client, from the app to their plan

What a client goes through today, traced through the code on 13 Nov and checked twice. Each step says who it is about and whether it works. The fixes, most important first, are at the bottom.

Before the app

The coach adds you, and the invite goes out

Finlay presses New clientWorks

Name, email, birthdate, phone and address, then Create and invite. Your account exists from that moment, with no password.

An invite email goes out at onceGap

Sent by Clerk with its own wording and look, not "Finlay invited you". The link works for 30 days. It goes the second you're added, before Finlay has built anything, and there's no way to send it again if it gets lost.

1
Getting the app

Finding Norcoach on the phone

Nothing points to the appGap

The invite has no App Store or TestFlight link, and the site never says there's an app. Today the client hears about it from the coach.

Tapping the invite opens the app, if it's installedNew today

Built today: the link opens Norcoach on the invite page. Without the app it opens Safari, and the client later has to sign in again inside the app. Not yet tried on a phone.

2
Getting in

Signing in, no password

From the invite
"Alex, Finlay invited you"Works

Finlay's face, one line about the app, then Continue with email. The link already proves the address, so no code to type. Apple and Google too.

From the app's sign-in
Email, then a 6-digit codeWorks

Type the email Finlay used, enter the code from the email, and you're in. Google and Apple work the same way.

Matched to Finlay's client by email aloneBroken

Google under another address, Apple's Hide My Email, or a typo in the email Finlay typed all land on No account yet. That screen also offers Create a coach account. Finlay isn't told any of it.

3
First open

The opening, then straight to Home

The opening playsWorks

The star on paper, NORCOACH coming out from behind it, then Home.

Nothing is asked: no notificationsGap

The app never asks for notifications. They only come on if the client finds Settings → Preferences. Until then, everything Finlay sends, and every call reminder, goes nowhere outside the app.

No welcome stepGap

No hello from the coach, no Apple Health ask, nothing about the gym. Coaches get a welcome when they join; clients don't.

4
The first Home

Personalised, or empty

Finlay built the plan first
Today, the cards, Finlay's latestWorks

Today lists the session (Start) and the check-in (Fill in). The phase cards show this week, and Latest from shows what Finlay set up, with To focus on once the first call is done.

Nothing built yet
"All set for today"Broken

A green check over nothing, three No plan yet cards and Nothing from Finlay yet. A brand-new client is told they're done before anything exists. Likely, because the invite leaves before the plan is built.

A plan that starts later starts silentlyGap

Its card says No plan yet with no date until the day comes; a training programme announces itself then, lifestyle and nutrition don't.

The welcome line can be wrongGap

With no other news, Finlay's spot reads "Your first session is ready. Check in each morning…", even on a nutrition-only start, and it explains a feature.

5
The first days

Training, food and the chat

Nutrition logs from day oneWorks

With no targets yet it's the food diary on its own, so food can be logged straight away.

Training is blank without a programmeGap

The photo banner and nothing under it, and no way to log a rogue session yet, so a keen client can't log what they train while the plan is coming.

First workout asks the gymWorks

Start always opens the gym picker; with no gyms it goes straight to adding one.

The chat is readyWorks

One line over an empty thread; a message reaches Finlay's phone.

Check-in reminders never arriveBroken

They're written but never shown or pushed, so the Check-in reminders switch does nothing. Call reminders are push only, so without notifications they're lost too.

6
Meanwhile, for the coach

Knowing the client is in

Only the rail tells himGap

The client's face loses its dashed ring once they've signed in, and the chat says Active now. No row in the Feed, no push, and App access says nothing about whether they're in.

What to fix, most important first

Ranked by how much each one hurts a new client's first impression.

1Send the invite when the plan is readyMedium

New client creates the account without sending anything; App access gets Send invite, and later Resend. Then the first Home is never empty, and a lost email can be sent again.

2Ask for notifications once, after the first HomeMedium

Inside the app, the phone's own question one time. Without it the coach's messages and call reminders never reach the phone.

3Our own invite email, from the coachSmall

"Finlay invited you to Norcoach", in the app's look, with the App Store (TestFlight for now) link and the invite button. Replies go to the coach.

Needs you: verify norcoachapp.com in Resend, so mail can come from it.

4Link the account through the invite, not the emailMedium

Whoever opens the invite becomes that client, whatever address Google or Apple gives. No more No account yet for a real client, and no coach link on that screen.

5App access tells the coach where things standSmall

Signed in or Not yet, when the invite went, and Resend. A Feed row when a client joins.

6An honest empty HomeSmall

No "All set for today" before there's a plan, and Finlay's spot without the explaining sentence.

7Reminders that arriveSmall

Check-in reminders pushed (or the switch removed), call reminders as a notification row as well as a push.

8Small clean-upsSmall

The invite link always built on app.norcoachapp.com (today it follows whatever address the coach is on), a broken invite page says sign in instead of "Hi, Your invited you", and the gym picker's two contradicting lines about moving sets.

Already done today: the invite opens the app when it's installed, the sign-in screen is cleaner (centred logo, no coach link inside the app), and Start always asks for the gym.
