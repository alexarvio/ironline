A to-do strip at the top of a tab

The rail's number and the tab's dot lead here. At the top of the tab, before anything else, is one row per thing to do on that tab: what it is, how long it has waited, and the action. Tapping the action does it, or opens the exact spot. When nothing is left, the strip is gone and the dots go with it. The colour is the rust the dots already use (between amber and red); shades are compared at the bottom.
