The empty meal card

A meal with no picture today is dark green-grey, which jumps hard against the page and the see-through tab bar. Three lighter ways, each with the tab bar over the cards as you scroll. A meal with a photo keeps its photo and white type in all of them.

My pick: A. The empty card reads as the page's own paper, so nothing flashes when you switch tabs, and a meal with a photo stands out as the thing that's filled in. B keeps a hint of nutrition's teal; C stays a "card" but softer.
