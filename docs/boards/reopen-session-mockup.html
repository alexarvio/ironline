Reopen a session

A client ended a session by mistake and wants it back. Today a finished session's ⋯ has only Edit workout. Three ways to fix that.

A · Reopen in the ⋯, asked in the menu Suggested

The finished session's ⋯ gets Reopen under Edit workout. It asks in the menu itself, the way Delete does in the chat. Once she confirms, the session is in progress again with every set kept, and the Resume pill is back on Training. Ending it again saves it as usual. The coach just sees it as in progress until it's ended again.
