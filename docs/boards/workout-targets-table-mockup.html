The workout's targets as a table: three ways

Lying Leg Curl with every column the coach can switch on: Sets, Reps, kg, RPE, Tempo and Rest (today Tempo and Rest never reach the workout screen; all three below show them). A column the coach leaves empty is left out, so a plain exercise is just Sets, Reps and kg. Hairline only, no boxes.

AOne row, labelled columns

A small table row: the column names in small caps over a hairline, the figures under them. Reads like the coach's own table. Six columns fit one line on a phone.

BA grid, three across

Each target its own cell in a hairline grid, the name over the figure, three a row. Two rows with six; one row with three. Most like a spec sheet.

CA list, name and figure

One target a line, the name on the left in grey, the figure on the right, a hairline between. The most room per figure, the tallest.
