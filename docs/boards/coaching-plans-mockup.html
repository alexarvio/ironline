Questions for Alex

  1. A price change. When a coach changes Online coaching from €150 to €170: (a) every client on it pays €170 from their next invoice, or (b) clients already on it keep €150 and only new ones pay €170? My pick: (b), coaches usually honour the old price; the Plans row would say `€170 | month` and a client on the old price shows `€150` on their series.
  2. Picking a plan. (a) The Invoices tab offers Start billing and the coach confirms (C), or (b) picking a priced plan starts the series right away from today. My pick: (a), nothing goes to a client before the coach confirms.
  3. Where plans live. A Settings section (A), or on the Business screen? My pick: Settings; Business stays numbers.
  4. What a plan holds. Name, price, how often. Anything else now (what's included as words on the invoice, a sign-up fee, a minimum term)? My pick: just the three for this round.
